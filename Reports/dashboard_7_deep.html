Batch Processing & Data Quality Dashboard

Overview

This dashboard provides an overview of recent batch processing activities and control rule checks for February 2026. The goal is to identify success rates, recurring issues, and areas for process improvement. The visualizations help track batch health over time, control rule failure patterns, and highlight key data quality concerns.

Batch Success Rate

Approximately 70% of batches executed successfully, indicating overall stability. However, 30% encountered failures or were blocked, mainly due to score thresholds or control rule violations. The trend over the month shows intermittent spikes in failures, suggesting periods requiring further investigation.

Control Rule Failures

The "C01_ROWCOUNT" and "C03_REFERENTIAL" controls are the most common failure points, often failing together, which indicates potential data inconsistencies or referential integrity issues in the source systems. Addressing these will improve overall data quality and batch reliability.
