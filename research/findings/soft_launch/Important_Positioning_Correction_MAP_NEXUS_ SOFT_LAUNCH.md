## IMPORTANT POSITIONING CORRECTION — MAP NEXUS SOFT LAUNCH

Before proceeding with Week 1 implementation, update the soft-launch strategy to reflect the actual product positioning.

MAP Nexus should be positioned as:

**MAP Nexus — Migration Assurance & Data Validation**

Do NOT position the product primarily or exclusively as:

**"Financial Migration Assurance"**

The previous financial-services positioning was too narrow. Financial services remains an important early target market because migration accuracy, reconciliation, governance and auditability are particularly important there, but it is NOT the definition or restriction of the MAP Nexus product.

### General product positioning

MAP Nexus is a Migration Assurance & Data Validation platform that helps organisations prove migrated data is:

* correct
* complete
* reconciled
* structurally valid
* ready for cutover

The core proposition is migration assurance and data validation, regardless of industry.

### Market positioning

Financial services may be used as an initial market-validation segment, but the public company positioning should remain broad enough to support other sectors.

Potential sectors include, but are not limited to:

* Financial services
* Pharmaceuticals
* Government
* Housing
* Insurance
* Healthcare
* Enterprise organisations
* Other organisations undertaking significant data migrations

Do not imply that MAP Nexus is a banking-only or financial-institutions-only product.

### Founder positioning

The founder profile should also reflect broader experience across financial services, pharmaceuticals, housing, government and other data/migration environments where appropriate.

The founder story should therefore focus on experience with:

**data + migration + analytics + data engineering + migration assurance**

rather than presenting the founder exclusively as a financial-services specialist.

### LinkedIn objective

The personal LinkedIn profile and MAP Nexus Company Page should work together:

**Edward Odewale — Founder / product builder**

↓ drives awareness, conversation and market learning

**MAP Nexus — Migration Assurance & Data Validation**

↓ official company/product presence

The Company Page should communicate the broader MAP Nexus product proposition.

Financial services can be mentioned as an early area of market validation, but should not appear to be the exclusive customer segment.

### Soft-launch objective

The purpose of the next four weeks is MARKET VALIDATION.

We are not trying to present MAP Nexus as a finished enterprise product.

We are trying to:

1. Put the product proposition in front of the market.
2. Start conversations with people responsible for data migration and validation.
3. Identify which industries and use cases respond most strongly.
4. Discover the most painful migration-assurance problems.
5. Secure early design-partner conversations.
6. Learn and refine the positioning based on real feedback.

Do not prematurely lock MAP Nexus into one industry before the market has told us where the strongest opportunity is.
