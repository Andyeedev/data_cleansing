How long does it take to get started?

Most institutions are up and running within 24-48 hours. Our onboarding team will guide you through the setup process, data migration, and staff training to ensure a smooth transition.

Can I migrate my existing data to EduFlow?

Absolutely! We provide comprehensive data migration services at no additional cost. Our team will securely transfer all your student records, staff information, attendance history, and other data from your current system.

Is my data secure?

Security is our top priority. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption, regular security audits, and are fully GDPR compliant. Our data centers are SOC 2 Type II certified with 99.9% uptime guarantee and daily backups.

Can I customize the platform for my institution?

Yes! EduFlow is highly customizable. You can customize user roles, permissions, workflows, reports, and even add your institution's branding. Enterprise customers can also request custom features and integrations.

What kind of training and support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive training including live onboarding sessions, video tutorials, detailed documentation, and ongoing webinars. Our support team is available 24/7 via email, phone, and live chat to assist you.
