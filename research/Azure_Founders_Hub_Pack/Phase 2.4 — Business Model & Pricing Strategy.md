# Phase 2.4 — Business Model & Pricing Strategy

## Executive Summary

The platform follows a **multi-sided SaaS + AI consumption model**.

Revenue is generated from four complementary sources:

1. Enterprise SaaS subscriptions
2. AI usage-based billing
3. Marketplace commissions
4. Enterprise implementation & premium services

This creates predictable recurring revenue while allowing expansion through AI consumption and ecosystem growth.
