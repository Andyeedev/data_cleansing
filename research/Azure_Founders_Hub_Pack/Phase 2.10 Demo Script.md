# Demo Scenarios

## Scenario 1 – Predictive Maintenance

A water pump begins to show increased vibration.

Platform:

* detects anomaly
* predicts bearing failure
* estimates remaining useful life
* recommends maintenance
* generates work order
* notifies engineering team

**Outcome:** Failure prevented before downtime occurs.

## Scenario 2 – Energy Optimisation

An office building experiences abnormal overnight energy consumption.

Platform:

* identifies unexpected usage
* traces the issue to HVAC equipment
* recommends corrective action
* estimates annual cost savings
* updates sustainability dashboard

**Outcome:** Reduced energy costs and carbon emissions.
