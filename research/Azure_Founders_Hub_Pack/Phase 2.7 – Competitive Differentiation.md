Excellent. This is one of the most important sections from an investor's perspective.

A common mistake startups make is saying, **"We have no competitors."** Investors immediately lose confidence. Instead, we need to demonstrate that we understand the competitive landscape and articulate why our platform is differentiated.

Given everything we've developed so far, I wouldn't position the company as competing with Microsoft, IBM, or Siemens.
