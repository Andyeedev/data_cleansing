Yes—it does matter, and I'm glad you raised it now.

Since this is a **UK-based company**, I recommend we standardise **all financial projections in GBP (£)** throughout the business plan and investor documentation.

There are several reasons for this:

* **Companies House** filings are in GBP.
* UK investors (angel investors, VCs, Innovate UK, British Business Bank, etc.) expect forecasts in GBP.
* HMRC, accounting, corporation tax and statutory accounts are all prepared in GBP.
* Your operating costs (salaries, office, legal, Azure UK South hosting, etc.) will largely be incurred in GBP.
* It demonstrates financial discipline and avoids unnecessary foreign exchange assumptions.

The only exception is when preparing material specifically for international investors (for example, US venture capital firms or Microsoft executives), where we can include a secondary USD conversion alongside GBP.

For example:

| Metric        | Preferred      |
| ------------- | -------------- |
| ARR           | **£1.2M**      |
| Funding Round | **£750k Seed** |
| Burn Rate     | **£45k/month** |
| Gross Margin  | 82%            |
| EBITDA        | £xxx           |

rather than

> $1.2M ARR
