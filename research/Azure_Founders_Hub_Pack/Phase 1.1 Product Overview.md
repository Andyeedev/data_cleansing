# Executive Summary

Modern enterprises continue to rely on legacy applications that have evolved over decades. While these systems often contain mission-critical business data and processes, they present significant barriers to cloud adoption, digital transformation, system integration, and AI enablement.

Traditional migration projects are expensive, labour-intensive, and highly dependent on manual analysis. Database discovery, schema comparison, data mapping, validation, and governance typically require months of specialist effort, increasing project costs and introducing substantial delivery risk.

Our platform addresses these challenges through an **AI-powered Enterprise Data Modernisation Platform** that automates the discovery, analysis, mapping, validation, and migration planning of enterprise data assets.

Rather than functioning as a conventional migration tool, the platform serves as an intelligent decision-support system that enables organisations to modernise legacy environments with greater speed, consistency, transparency, and confidence.

---

# The Problem

Large organisations face several common challenges when modernising legacy systems:

* Incomplete or outdated system documentation.
* Complex and inconsistent database schemas.
* Manual data mapping processes.
* High migration costs.
* Limited visibility into migration risks.
* Poor governance and auditability.
* Difficulty leveraging Artificial Intelligence due to fragmented data landscapes.

These challenges often delay cloud transformation initiatives and increase operational risk.
