# Strategic Principles

Throughout the roadmap, development will be guided by the following principles:

* **Microsoft-first architecture** – Continue leveraging Azure, Azure AI, Fabric, and the Microsoft ecosystem to reduce integration complexity.
* **Security and compliance by design** – Embed governance, Zero Trust, and regulatory readiness into every release.
* **Composable platform** – Deliver modular capabilities that allow customers to adopt functionality at their own pace.
* **AI with human oversight** – Ensure AI augments operational decision-making while maintaining transparency, explainability, and governance.
