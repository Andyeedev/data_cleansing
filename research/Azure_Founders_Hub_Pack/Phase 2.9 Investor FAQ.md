# Phase 2.9 – Investor FAQ

## Purpose

Prepare clear and credible answers to the questions most commonly asked during investor meetings, pitch events, grant assessments, and due diligence.

# Company & Vision

### 1. What problem are you solving?

Organisations operating complex physical infrastructure often manage data across disconnected systems, making it difficult to gain real-time operational insight or make informed decisions quickly.

Our platform unifies IoT data, Digital Twins, AI, and predictive analytics into a single Operational Intelligence Platform that enables organisations to monitor assets, predict failures, optimise performance, reduce costs, and improve sustainability.

---

### 2. Why is this problem important now?

Several trends are driving demand:

* Accelerated digital transformation
* Increased adoption of AI across enterprise operations
* Ageing infrastructure requiring predictive maintenance
* Rising energy costs
* Regulatory focus on ESG and sustainability
* Greater reliance on connected assets and IoT

Together, these create a strong need for intelligent operational platforms that move organisations from reactive to predictive management.

---

### 3. Why will customers buy from you instead of building it themselves?

Building an equivalent platform requires expertise across cloud architecture, AI, IoT, Digital Twins, cybersecurity, data engineering, and enterprise integration.

Our platform offers:

* Faster deployment
* Lower implementation costs
* Proven Microsoft-native architecture
* Continuous product innovation
* Reduced technical risk
* Ongoing support and feature development

Customers benefit from a ready-to-deploy solution without the complexity of developing and maintaining their own platform.

---

# Market Opportunity

### 4. How large is the opportunity?

The addressable market spans organisations managing complex assets across sectors such as utilities, healthcare, manufacturing, transport, local government, commercial property, and financial services.

Our strategy begins with UK enterprise customers before expanding into European markets and, over time, globally through the Microsoft ecosystem and strategic partnerships.

---

### 5. Why start in the UK?

The UK offers:

* Strong government support for digital innovation
* Mature cloud adoption
* High concentration of regulated industries
* Active funding through Innovate UK and regional growth programmes
* A well-established Microsoft partner ecosystem

Establishing traction domestically provides a strong foundation for international expansion.
