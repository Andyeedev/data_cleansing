Six Integrated Solutions

Each solution addresses a critical aspect of migration assurance, working together to deliver comprehensive programme visibility and control.

Migration Assurance

The core platform providing end-to-end lifecycle governance for your entire migration programme.

  • End-to-end lifecycle governance from discovery to decommission
  • Central control plane for all migration activities and stakeholders
  • Automated evidence collection and audit trail generation
  • Real-time risk tracking and escalation workflows

Migration Governance

Enforce policies, maintain compliance, and ensure adherence to migration standards throughout.

  • Policy enforcement with automated compliance checks
  • Complete audit trails for every migration action
  • Compliance dashboards with real-time status reporting
  • Role-based access control with granular permissions

Migration Validation

Comprehensive testing and validation to ensure migrated applications meet quality standards.

  • Automated functional testing across migration waves
  • Performance benchmarking with baseline comparisons
  • Data integrity verification and reconciliation
  • Quality gates with automated pass/fail criteria

Executive Reporting

Powerful dashboards and reports designed for C-suite visibility and programme governance.

  • C-suite dashboards with real-time programme health
  • Programme-level reporting with drill-down capabilities
  • Customisable templates for different stakeholder needs
  • Multiple export formats including PDF, Excel, and API

Delivery Assurance

Track delivery progress, manage milestones, and identify blockers before they impact timelines.

  • Sprint and iteration tracking with velocity metrics
  • Milestone management with dependency mapping
  • Early blocker identification and escalation paths
  • Risk scoring with automated impact assessment

Programme Intelligence

AI-powered analytics providing predictive insights and actionable recommendations.

  • Predictive risk analysis using machine learning
  • Natural language querying for instant insights
  • Trend detection across migration waves and programmes
  • AI-powered recommendations for optimisation
