🔷 Founder Narrative (Polished Version)

After working across financial data migrations, I repeatedly saw the same pattern:

Massive investment in transformation.
Minimal investment in structured validation.

Migration validation was fragmented — Excel reconciliations, ad-hoc SQL, consultant-built scripts, and manual review.

Yet boards were signing off on go-live decisions without a structured, scored, regulator-ready assurance framework.

I realised there was no formalised migration validation standard.

So I built one.

The Migration Validation Engine is designed to provide:

Control-driven validation

Board-level confidence scoring

Regulator-ready audit traceability

Docker-based deployability inside secure environments

Not just to detect defects.

But to certify migration confidence.
