# Executive Summary

Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) is an AI-powered enterprise data modernisation platform designed to reduce the risk, cost, and complexity associated with legacy system transformation.

Organisations across financial services and other regulated industries continue to invest heavily in modernisation programmes, cloud migration initiatives, core system replacements, and data transformation projects. Despite these investments, migration failures remain one of the largest sources of project delays, operational disruption, regulatory exposure, and financial loss.

MAP addresses this challenge by providing an intelligent platform that automates migration discovery, mapping, validation, governance, and migration planning.

Rather than functioning as a traditional ETL or migration utility, MAP acts as the assurance and intelligence layer that helps organisations modernise legacy environments with greater confidence, transparency, and control.

---

# The Problem We Are Solving

Large organisations often operate complex legacy systems that have evolved over decades.

These environments typically suffer from:

* Incomplete documentation
* Poor visibility of dependencies
* Manual mapping processes
* Inconsistent validation approaches
* Limited governance
* High migration risk
* Significant project costs

When organisations attempt to modernise these systems, migration teams spend substantial time manually analysing schemas, comparing structures, validating data, documenting mappings, and managing migration governance.

This process is expensive, time-consuming, and highly dependent on specialist knowledge.

The result is that many transformation projects experience delays, budget overruns, and avoidable risks.

# Our Solution

Migration Assurance Platform provides an integrated environment for enterprise migration assurance.

The platform combines:

### Discovery

Automated extraction of metadata from legacy and target systems.

---

### Mapping

Intelligent schema comparison and mapping recommendations.

---

### Validation

Automated migration assurance controls that verify migration quality.

---

### Governance

Approval workflows, audit history, and migration traceability.

---

### Migration Intelligence

Readiness assessments, risk identification, and migration planning.

---

The objective is to reduce manual effort while improving consistency, governance, and migration confidence.
