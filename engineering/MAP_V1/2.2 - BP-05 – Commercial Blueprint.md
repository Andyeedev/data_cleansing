# 1. Purpose

The Risk & Assurance Blueprint establishes the standard methodology for identifying, assessing, governing, monitoring, and mitigating risks throughout the migration lifecycle.

It ensures that migration programmes maintain appropriate governance, comply with organizational and regulatory requirements, and provide executives with confidence that risks are actively managed through evidence-based assurance.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Risk & Assurance Blueprint aims to:

* Establish a consistent risk management framework.
* Identify and assess migration-related risks.
* Monitor risk exposure throughout the programme.
* Define assurance activities and governance controls.
* Support executive decision-making with risk intelligence.
* Ensure compliance with internal and external requirements.
* Reduce the likelihood and impact of migration failures.
* Maintain an auditable record of risk decisions and assurance outcomes.

# 7. Risk Assessment Matrix

Each identified risk is evaluated using probability and impact.

| Probability | Definition     |
| ----------- | -------------- |
| 1           | Rare           |
| 2           | Unlikely       |
| 3           | Possible       |
| 4           | Likely         |
| 5           | Almost Certain |

| Impact | Definition |
| ------ | ---------- |
| 1      | Negligible |
| 2      | Minor      |
| 3      | Moderate   |
| 4      | Major      |
| 5      | Critical   |

**Risk Score = Probability × Impact**

### Risk Ratings

| Score | Rating   | Action               |
| ----: | -------- | -------------------- |
|   1–5 | Low      | Monitor              |
|  6–10 | Medium   | Mitigation Required  |
| 11–15 | High     | Management Attention |
| 16–25 | Critical | Executive Escalation |

# 12. Risk Treatment Strategies

Each risk must have an agreed response.

| Strategy | Description                           |
| -------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Avoid    | Eliminate the source of the risk      |
| Mitigate | Reduce probability or impact          |
| Transfer | Shift responsibility to another party |
| Accept   | Acknowledge and monitor the risk      |

Every treatment must include:

* Assigned owner
* Due date
* Success criteria
* Residual risk assessment

# 17. Best Practices

* Review risks continuously, not only at milestones.
* Base every risk decision on objective evidence.
* Escalate critical risks early.
* Link risks directly to business outcomes.
* Integrate risk discussions into governance forums.
* Use dashboards to communicate trends, not just current status.
* Capture lessons learned for future engagements.
