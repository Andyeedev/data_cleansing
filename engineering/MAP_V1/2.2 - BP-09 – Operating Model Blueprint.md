# 1. Purpose

The Operating Model Blueprint defines how the organization creates, delivers, operates, and continuously improves products and services.

It establishes:

* Organizational operating principles
* Product-centric ways of working
* Decision rights
* Governance integration
* Delivery operating model
* Funding model
* Performance management
* Continuous improvement framework

The blueprint serves as the operational design for the Product Operating System (POS).

---

# 2. Objectives

The Operating Model enables the organization to:

* Deliver customer value faster
* Improve cross-functional collaboration
* Increase strategic alignment
* Reduce operational friction
* Improve decision quality
* Standardize execution
* Enable continuous delivery
* Scale product management consistently

---

# 3. Operating Principles

The Product Operating Model is guided by the following principles.

### Customer First

Every decision should improve customer outcomes.

### Product over Project

Products are continuously managed assets rather than temporary initiatives.

### Outcome Driven

Success is measured by outcomes rather than outputs.

### Empowered Teams

Decision-making occurs as close as possible to the work.

### Data Driven

Operational and strategic decisions are supported by measurable evidence.

### Continuous Learning

Feedback loops drive ongoing improvement.

### Governance without Bureaucracy

Governance provides clarity and control while minimizing unnecessary overhead.

### Transparency

Information, priorities, and decisions are visible across the organization.

# 8. Planning Cadence

Planning occurs across multiple horizons.

| Horizon                     | Frequency           | Purpose                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Strategic Planning          | Annual              | Long-term direction       |
| Portfolio Planning          | Quarterly           | Investment prioritization |
| Product Planning            | Quarterly / Monthly | Product roadmap           |
| Release Planning            | Monthly             | Delivery coordination     |
| Sprint / Iteration Planning | Every 2–4 Weeks     | Execution planning        |
| Daily Planning              | Daily               | Team coordination         |

# 16. Operating Model Maturity

Organizations mature through five levels.

| Level        | Description                                    |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Initial   | Project-centric, ad hoc execution              |
| 2. Emerging  | Basic product management practices             |
| 3. Defined   | Standardized operating model                   |
| 4. Managed   | Metrics-driven optimization                    |
| 5. Optimized | Continuous improvement and adaptive governance |

# 17. Success Measures

The Operating Model is successful when:

* Strategy consistently translates into execution.
* Product teams operate with clear ownership.
* Governance accelerates rather than delays decisions.
* Funding aligns with product value.
* Delivery is predictable and measurable.
* Customer outcomes improve continuously.
* Technology and business remain aligned.
* Continuous improvement becomes part of organizational culture.
