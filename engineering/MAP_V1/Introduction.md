# What we should do next

I don't think we should jump straight into writing the Product Canon.

Instead, I'd spend one session designing it properly.

Specifically:

## Step 1 — Design the Canon (its structure)

Not writing content yet.

We agree on:

* Sections
* Purpose of each section
* Inputs (where the information comes from)
* Outputs (which documents consume that information)

This is essentially the blueprint for the Canon.

---

## Step 2 — Build the Canon

One section at a time.

For example:

1. Company Identity
2. Vision & Mission
3. Problem Statement
4. Solution Statement
5. Platform Definition
6. Current Capabilities
7. Roadmap
8. Customer Segments
9. Value Proposition
10. Commercial Strategy
11. Azure Alignment
12. Technical Principles
13. Product Maturity
14. Canonical Terminology

Each section is reviewed and approved before moving to the next.

---

## Step 3 — Lock the Canon

Once complete, we version it as:

**Product Canon v1.0**

From that point onward, it becomes read-only except for controlled updates when the product evolves.

---

## Step 4 — Generate Deliverables

With the Canon in place, creating application packs becomes a straightforward process:

* Microsoft Founders Hub Pack
* AWS Activate Pack
* Google for Startups Pack
* NVIDIA Inception Pack
* Investor Pitch Deck
* Sales Deck
* Website Content
* Partner Briefings

Each of these references the Canon rather than reinventing the story.

---

## My recommendation

I think we should treat the **Product Canon v1.0** as the **architectural foundation of Phase 2**, not as one of the deliverables.

In other words:

* **Phase 2.1** = Build and approve the Product Canon.
* **Phase 2.2 onwards** = Generate every external-facing pack from that Canon.

That approach gives us consistency, traceability, and a single source of truth. It also aligns perfectly with how we've approached the engineering side of the project: establish the core architecture first, then build reusable components on top of it.

I think this will be one of the highest-value assets we create because it becomes the reference point for everything that follows.
