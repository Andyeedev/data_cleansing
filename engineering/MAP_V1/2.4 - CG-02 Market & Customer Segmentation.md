# 2. Market Definition

MAP operates within the broader market of:

### Enterprise Data Modernisation

Sub-markets include:

* Data Migration
* System Modernisation
* Core Platform Replacement
* Cloud Transformation
* Data Governance
* Data Validation

---

## MAP Market Category

MAP creates a more specialised category:

> Migration Assurance

A category focused specifically on validating and governing data migrations before production deployment.

---

# 3. Market Drivers

Several global trends are increasing demand for migration assurance solutions.

---

## Cloud Transformation

Organisations continue migrating from:

* On-premise environments
* Legacy infrastructure
* Proprietary platforms

towards:

* Azure
* AWS
* Hybrid Cloud

Every cloud migration introduces data migration risk.

---

## Legacy System Replacement

Many critical systems are 15–30 years old.

Examples include:

* Core Banking Systems
* Insurance Platforms
* Regulatory Reporting Platforms
* Data Warehouses

Modernisation initiatives require extensive migration programmes.

---

## Regulatory Pressure

Regulated industries increasingly require:

* Audit evidence
* Data lineage
* Traceability
* Governance

Migration assurance becomes a compliance requirement.

---

## AI Readiness

Organisations cannot deploy AI effectively without trusted data.

Migration assurance becomes a foundational capability for AI adoption.

---

# 6. Customer Segmentation Model

MAP customers are segmented using four tiers.

---

# Tier 1 — Enterprise Customers

Large regulated organisations.

Characteristics:

* Complex technology estates
* Multiple migration programmes
* High compliance requirements

---

Examples:

* Global Banks
* Large Insurers
* Financial Market Infrastructure Providers

---

### Customer Needs

* Migration assurance
* Governance
* Auditability
* Risk reduction

---

# Tier 2 — Mid-Market Customers

Medium-sized organisations.

Characteristics:

* Smaller internal teams
* Active transformation programmes
* Limited migration tooling

---

Examples:

* Regional Banks
* Building Societies
* Mid-sized Insurers

---

### Customer Needs

* Reduced migration risk
* Faster validation
* Simplified governance

---

# Tier 3 — Delivery Partners

Organisations delivering migration programmes.

Examples:

* System Integrators
* Consulting Firms
* Migration Specialists

---

### Customer Needs

* Repeatable delivery
* Reduced project risk
* Audit evidence

---

# Tier 4 — Cloud Ecosystem Partners

Technology providers supporting transformation programmes.

Examples:

* Microsoft
* AWS
* Azure Partners
* Consulting Partners

---

### Customer Needs

* Successful customer migrations
* Reduced migration failures
* Increased cloud adoption

---
