# 1. Purpose

The Discovery Template Library provides a standardized collection of templates used to understand customer needs, validate opportunities, assess migration challenges, and define problem statements before product planning and delivery.

The templates ensure that product decisions are based on evidence, stakeholder engagement, and measurable business outcomes rather than assumptions.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Discovery Template Library aims to:

* Standardize discovery activities.
* Capture customer and stakeholder insights.
* Validate migration and transformation opportunities.
* Reduce delivery risk through early assessment.
* Improve strategic alignment.
* Support investment decisions with evidence.
* Create traceability from discovery to delivery.

---

# 3. Scope

The templates support the early stages of the Product Operating System lifecycle, including:

* Customer discovery
* Stakeholder engagement
* Opportunity assessment
* Business problem definition
* Research
* Hypothesis validation
* Solution exploration
* Migration readiness assessment
* Strategic alignment

# 5. Standard Template Structure

Every discovery template should contain the following sections where applicable:

## Document Information

* Template ID
* Template Name
* Version
* Owner
* Approval Status
* Effective Date
* Review Date

---

## Purpose

Defines why the template is used.

---

## When to Use

Specifies the point in the discovery lifecycle when the template should be completed.

---

## Intended Audience

Typical users include:

* Product Managers
* Business Analysts
* Customer Success Managers
* Solution Architects
* Enterprise Architects
* Delivery Leads
* Sales Engineers
* Executive Sponsors

---

## Required Inputs

Supporting information that should exist before completing the template.

Examples include:

* Customer objectives
* Existing architecture
* Business strategy
* Interview notes
* Assessment reports
* Regulatory requirements

---

## Instructions

Provides guidance on how to complete the template, mandatory fields, and examples where appropriate.

---

## Template Body

The structured fields, prompts, checklists, tables, or canvases to capture discovery outputs.

---

## Review & Approval

Identifies required reviewers, approvers, and governance checkpoints.

---

## Related Documents

References associated Product Canon, Blueprint, Strategy Templates, Planning Templates, and Governance Templates.

# 6. Discovery Workflow

The standard discovery process is:

1. Define business context
2. Identify stakeholders
3. Conduct interviews and workshops
4. Assess current state
5. Validate business problems
6. Identify opportunities
7. Assess migration readiness
8. Evaluate risks and constraints
9. Define future state vision
10. Prioritise opportunities
11. Produce discovery summary
12. Obtain governance approval

---

# 7. Design Principles

Discovery templates should be:

* Evidence-based
* Customer-centric
* Outcome-focused
* Easy to complete
* Reusable
* Consistent
* Traceable
* Governance-ready
* Collaborative
* Vendor-neutral

---

# 8. Governance

The Product Strategy Office is responsible for:

* Maintaining discovery templates.
* Reviewing updates.
* Ensuring alignment with the Product Canon.
* Publishing approved versions.
* Retiring obsolete templates.

# 10. Template Relationships

Discovery Templates support and feed:

* Strategy Templates (TR-02)
* Planning Templates (TR-04)
* Governance Templates (TR-06)
* Portfolio Templates (TR-11)
* Product Canon (PC-01 to PC-06)
* Blueprint Library (BP-01 to BP-10)

Outputs from Discovery become mandatory inputs for planning, delivery, governance, and portfolio investment decisions.

---

# 11. Success Measures

The Discovery Template Library is successful when:

* Discovery activities are performed consistently.
* Customer evidence is documented before investment decisions.
* Migration opportunities are validated objectively.
* Stakeholder engagement is traceable.
* Risks are identified early.
* Product planning is informed by verified discovery outputs.
* Governance decisions are supported by documented evidence.
