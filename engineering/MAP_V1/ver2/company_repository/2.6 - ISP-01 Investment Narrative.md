# Executive Summary

Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) exists to solve one of the most expensive and least addressed challenges in enterprise technology:

> The inability to modernise legacy systems with confidence.

Every year organisations invest billions in:

* Core banking transformation
* Cloud migration
* Data modernisation
* Digital transformation
* Platform replacement programmes

Yet migration remains one of the highest-risk activities in technology delivery.

MAP is being built to become the assurance and intelligence layer for enterprise modernisation.

Our vision is to create the platform organisations trust before, during, and after every major migration.

---

# The Problem

Enterprise technology landscapes have become increasingly complex.

Most organisations operate hundreds of systems containing:

* Business-critical data
* Regulatory obligations
* Operational processes
* Customer records

When these systems require replacement, transformation, or migration, organisations face a common set of challenges.

---

## Discovery Problems

Many organisations do not fully understand:

* What systems exist
* How systems connect
* What data is used
* Which dependencies matter

This creates significant uncertainty before migration projects begin.

---

## Mapping Problems

Migration teams spend months manually creating:

* Data mappings
* Transformation logic
* Validation rules

These activities are expensive, time-consuming, and difficult to govern.

---

## Validation Problems

Most migrations still rely on:

* Spreadsheets
* SQL scripts
* Manual checking
* Project-specific tooling

This creates operational and regulatory risk.

---

## Governance Problems

Few organisations possess a complete audit trail demonstrating:

* What changed
* Why it changed
* Who approved it
* Whether it was validated
