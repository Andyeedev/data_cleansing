# 5. Product Boundary

The Product Canon confirms MAP is **not**:

* a migration tool
* a backup platform
* a monitoring system
* a cloud provider
* a project management application

MAP is an **Assurance Platform** that orchestrates governance, validation, evidence, and decision support across migration programmes.

# 7. Design Principles

The Product Canon adopts the following guiding principles:

### Business First

Technology supports business outcomes.

---

### Evidence Driven

Every decision must be backed by measurable evidence.

---

### Vendor Neutral

MAP supports multiple technologies and cloud providers.

---

### Repeatable

Every engagement follows standardised methods.

---

### Scalable

Applicable to projects ranging from small migrations to global enterprise transformations.

---

### Secure by Design

Security is embedded into all product components and processes.

---

### Automation First

Automation is preferred wherever it improves consistency, quality, or efficiency.

---

### AI Assisted

Artificial intelligence enhances decision-making while maintaining human oversight.
