# 1. Purpose

This document defines the commercial pricing model and revenue strategy for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The objective is to establish a pricing structure that:

* Aligns with customer value
* Supports enterprise adoption
* Enables predictable recurring revenue
* Scales from pilot engagements to enterprise-wide deployment
* Supports future SaaS offerings

---

# 2. Pricing Philosophy

MAP is not sold as:

* A database connector
* A migration utility
* A data quality tool

MAP is sold as:

> A Migration Assurance Platform that reduces migration risk, improves governance, and provides audit-ready validation evidence.

Customers are purchasing:

* Risk reduction
* Faster migration delivery
* Regulatory confidence
* Governance controls
* Auditability

Pricing should therefore be based on business value rather than technical metrics alone.

# 5. Licensing Model

MAP should initially use annual subscription licensing.

Benefits:

* Predictable revenue
* Enterprise familiarity
* Easier budgeting

---

## Licence Basis

Recommended:

### Organisation Licence

Not:

* Per user
* Per database
* Per table

Reason:

Migration projects vary significantly.

Customers should focus on programme delivery rather than licence consumption.

# 12. Customer ROI Model

MAP pricing must always be positioned against migration risk.

---

## Example

A core banking migration:

Programme Cost:

£5M–£50M+

---

Migration Delay:

Potential cost:

£100K–£1M+ per week

---

Production Failure:

Potential impact:

Millions of pounds

---

MAP Cost:

Typically:

Less than 1% of programme value.

---

## Commercial Message

> MAP is significantly cheaper than migration failure.
