# MVP-01 – MVP Strategy & Scope

## Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)

Version 1.0
Status: Complete

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) strategy and scope for Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The MVP establishes the first production release of MAP and provides the foundation for:

* Product development
* Azure architecture
* User experience design
* Delivery planning
* Pilot customer engagement
* Future roadmap expansion

The objective is to deliver a focused solution that provides immediate customer value while validating market demand and platform direction.

---

# MVP Vision

## MVP Mission

Deliver the first operational version of MAP capable of providing migration governance, mapping, validation, and assurance capabilities for enterprise transformation programmes.

The MVP is designed to answer one critical question:

> Will organisations adopt MAP as their migration assurance platform?

# Conclusion

The MAP MVP establishes the minimum feature set required to validate the platform in real customer environments.

The MVP intentionally focuses on:

* Discovery
* Mapping
* Validation
* Governance
* Reporting

while deferring advanced AI and intelligence capabilities until product-market fit has been demonstrated.

This strategy maximises learning, reduces delivery risk, and provides a strong foundation for future platform evolution.
