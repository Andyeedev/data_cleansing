# 1. Purpose

This document defines the partner ecosystem strategy for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The objective is to create a scalable growth model by leveraging strategic partners rather than relying solely on direct sales.

The ecosystem strategy supports:

* Customer acquisition
* Market credibility
* Delivery scale
* Microsoft ecosystem alignment
* Future marketplace growth

---

# 2. Strategic Principle

MAP is not intended to become:

* A large consulting organisation
* A systems integrator
* A migration delivery company

Instead, MAP should become:

> The assurance platform used by organisations and partners delivering migration programmes.

This distinction is critical.

Partners deliver migrations.

MAP assures migrations.

---

# 3. Ecosystem Vision

The long-term vision is to position MAP at the centre of enterprise modernisation programmes.

---

### Enterprise Customer

Needs migration assurance.

↓

### Delivery Partner

Executes migration.

↓

### Cloud Provider

Provides infrastructure.

↓

### MAP

Provides validation, governance and assurance.

---

This creates a highly scalable ecosystem model.
