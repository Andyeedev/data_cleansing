# ISP-05 – Strategic Partnership Framework

## Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)

Version 1.0
Status: Complete

---

# Purpose

This document defines the strategic partnership model for Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The objective is to create a scalable ecosystem that accelerates:

* Market access
* Customer acquisition
* Platform adoption
* Solution delivery
* Industry credibility
* Revenue growth

Partnerships are viewed as a strategic growth engine rather than a supporting function.

---

# Partnership Vision

## Long-Term Goal

Build a global ecosystem where MAP becomes the recognised assurance and intelligence layer used alongside major enterprise transformation programmes.

The partnership strategy is designed to position MAP as:

> The trusted migration assurance platform embedded within enterprise modernisation ecosystems.

---

## Objective 2

# Partnership Principles

All partnerships should satisfy one or more of the following:

### Revenue Growth

Create direct commercial opportunities.

### Customer Access

Provide access to target customers.

### Delivery Capability

Increase implementation capacity.

# Risks

## Dependency Risk

Overreliance on a small number of partners.

Mitigation:

Diversified ecosystem.

---

## Channel Conflict

Potential overlap between direct and partner sales.

Mitigation:

Defined engagement rules.

---

## Enablement Risk

Partners unable to effectively position MAP.

Mitigation:

Structured enablement programme.

# Conclusion

MAP's growth strategy is ecosystem-led.

The objective is not to build every capability internally.

Instead, MAP will leverage partnerships to:

* Expand reach
* Accelerate adoption
* Increase credibility
* Improve delivery capacity
* Support long-term growth

Through a combination of Microsoft alignment, SI relationships, industry engagement, and cloud partnerships, MAP aims to become the recognised migration assurance platform used across enterprise transformation programmes.
