**Status:** Complete

---

# 1. Purpose

The Risk & Compliance Templates provide standardised artefacts for identifying, assessing, managing, governing, and reporting risks throughout a data migration or modernisation programme.

These templates ensure that:

* Migration risks are identified early.
* Decisions are documented.
* Regulatory obligations are tracked.
* Audit evidence is retained.
* Executive stakeholders receive consistent reporting.
* Programme governance remains defensible.
