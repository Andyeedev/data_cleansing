# Executive Summary

## Partner Opportunity

Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) is seeking strategic partnerships with leading system integrators (SIs) and consulting organisations to enhance enterprise transformation delivery.

MAP is designed to complement—not compete with—system integrators by providing the migration assurance, governance, validation, and intelligence capabilities that are often missing from large-scale transformation programmes.

Our objective is to help SIs:

* Reduce delivery risk
* Improve programme outcomes
* Increase customer confidence
* Strengthen governance
* Create new service opportunities
* Accelerate cloud adoption initiatives

---

# Why This Matters

Enterprise transformation programmes continue to increase in complexity.

System integrators are leading initiatives involving:

* Core banking modernisation
* Insurance platform replacement
* Cloud migration
* Data transformation
* Regulatory remediation
* Enterprise application migration

While migration execution tooling has evolved significantly, migration assurance capabilities remain fragmented.

This creates delivery challenges for both customers and delivery partners.

---

# The Challenge Facing System Integrators

Large transformation programmes commonly experience:

### Poor System Visibility

Limited understanding of dependencies and metadata.

---

### Mapping Complexity

Large volumes of migration mappings and business rules.

---

### Validation Challenges

Heavy reliance on manual validation activities.

---

### Governance Gaps

Insufficient traceability and auditability.

---

### Programme Risk

Increased probability of delays, defects, and rework.

---

### Knowledge Loss

Critical migration knowledge disappears after programme completion.
