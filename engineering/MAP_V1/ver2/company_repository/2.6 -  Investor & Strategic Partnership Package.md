# Phase 2.6 – Investor & Strategic Partnership Package

**Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)**

---

# Phase Objective

Phase 2.5 proved:

> "Why Microsoft should support MAP."

Phase 2.6 shifts focus to:

> "Why investors, strategic partners, system integrators, banks, insurers, and enterprise customers should engage with MAP."

This phase converts the Product Canon, Commercial Framework, and Founder Hub package into externally facing investment and partnership assets.
