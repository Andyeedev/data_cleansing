**Version:** 1.0

# 1. Purpose

The Portfolio Templates provide a standardised framework for managing multiple migration programmes, transformation initiatives, and client engagements across the Migration Assurance Platform ecosystem.

These templates ensure:

* Consistent portfolio governance
* Executive visibility
* Strategic alignment
* Resource optimisation
* Investment prioritisation
* Programme dependency management
* Benefits realisation tracking

---
