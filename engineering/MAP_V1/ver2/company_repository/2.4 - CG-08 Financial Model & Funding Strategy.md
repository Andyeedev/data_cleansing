# Purpose

This document defines the financial growth strategy, funding approach, capital requirements, and commercial assumptions supporting the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The objective is not to produce speculative venture-capital projections, but to establish a realistic financial framework aligned with the current maturity of the platform and the roadmap defined in the Product Canon.

---

# Financial Strategy Principles

MAP is being developed using five guiding principles:

### 1. Product Before Scale

The immediate priority is completing the core platform roadmap rather than premature expansion.

Investment will focus on:

* Discovery capabilities
* Mapping capabilities
* Validation capabilities
* Governance capabilities
* AI-assisted migration intelligence

---

### 2. Capital Efficient Growth

The platform is being developed with a strong emphasis on capital efficiency.

Objectives include:

* Maximising cloud credits
* Leveraging strategic partnerships
* Minimising infrastructure costs
* Building reusable platform capabilities

---

### 3. SaaS First

The preferred commercial model is subscription-based recurring revenue.

This creates:

* Predictable cash flow
* Scalable growth
* Higher business valuation
* Lower dependency on consulting revenue

---

### 4. Services Support Product

Professional services may accelerate adoption.

However:

Services support the platform.

The platform is not intended to become a consulting business.

---

### 5. Sustainable Expansion

Growth should be aligned with:

* Product maturity
* Customer demand
* Operational capacity

# Funding Narrative

MAP does not require significant upfront capital to validate the market.

The combination of:

* Existing platform assets
* Existing architecture
* Microsoft support
* Pilot customer engagements

creates a capital-efficient path toward commercialisation.

The strategy is therefore:

**Validate → Generate Revenue → Scale**

rather than

**Raise Capital → Build → Hope for Adoption**
