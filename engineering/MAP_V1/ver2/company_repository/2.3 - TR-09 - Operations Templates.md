# TR-09 – Operations Templates

**Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)**
**Phase 2.3 – Template & Reference Library**

**Document ID:** TR-09
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Complete

---

# 1. Purpose

The Operations Templates provide the standard operational artefacts required to run, support, monitor, govern, and continuously improve the Migration Assurance Platform.

These templates support:

* Internal MAP operations
* SaaS operations
* Client support
* Service management
* Release management
* Platform administration
* Incident management
* Operational governance
