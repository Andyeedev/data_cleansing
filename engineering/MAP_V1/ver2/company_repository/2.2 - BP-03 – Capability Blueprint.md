# 1. Purpose

The Discovery Blueprint establishes the standard methodology for identifying, collecting, validating, and documenting all information required to support a migration assurance engagement.

Discovery is the factual foundation of MAP. Every assessment, recommendation, risk evaluation, migration plan, and assurance decision relies on the quality and completeness of the discovery process.

The blueprint ensures that all relevant business, technical, operational, security, and governance information is captured in a structured, repeatable, and auditable manner.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Discovery Blueprint aims to:

* Establish a complete view of the current environment.
* Identify business and technical dependencies.
* Build a trusted evidence repository.
* Validate the accuracy of existing documentation.
* Discover hidden risks and constraints.
* Create an inventory of migration assets.
* Support readiness and planning activities.
* Provide traceable evidence for assurance decisions.

---

# 16. Best Practices

* Validate automated discovery with SMEs.
* Document assumptions explicitly.
* Assign ownership for every discovered asset.
* Record source and date for every evidence item.
* Maintain a single source of truth.
* Reconcile discrepancies before reporting.
* Prioritize critical business services.
