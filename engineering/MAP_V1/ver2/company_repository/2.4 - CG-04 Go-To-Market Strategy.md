**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Complete

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) will enter the market, acquire customers, establish credibility, and scale revenue.

The strategy aligns directly with:

* Product Canon
* Blueprint Library
* Microsoft Founders Hub objectives
* Future Azure Marketplace positioning

---

# 2. Go-To-Market Vision

MAP is not being positioned as:

* A migration tool
* A data quality platform
* A consultancy service

MAP is being positioned as:

> The Migration Assurance Platform for regulated enterprise transformation programmes.

The goal is to become a mandatory validation and governance layer that organisations use before migration go-live approval.

---

# 3. Market Entry Principles

The initial GTM strategy follows five principles:

### Principle 1 — Focused Market Entry

Start narrow.

Win a specific market before expanding.

---

### Principle 2 — Solve Expensive Problems

Target organisations where migration failure creates:

* Regulatory exposure
* Operational risk
* Financial loss

---

### Principle 3 — Evidence Before Scale

Build:

* Customer references
* Case studies
* Validation evidence

before aggressive expansion.

---

### Principle 4 — Leverage Ecosystems

Grow through:

* Microsoft
* Azure Partners
* System Integrators

rather than building a large direct sales force.

---

### Principle 5 — Product-Led Credibility

Lead conversations through:

* Demonstrations
* Proof of concepts
* Governance outcomes

rather than marketing claims.

---
