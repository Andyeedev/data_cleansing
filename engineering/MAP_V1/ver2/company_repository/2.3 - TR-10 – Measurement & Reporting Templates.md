# TR-10 – Measurement & Reporting Templates

**Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)**
**Phase 2.3 – Template & Reference Library**

**Document ID:** TR-10
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Complete

---

# 1. Purpose

The Measurement & Reporting Templates establish the standard framework for measuring, monitoring, reporting, and communicating the performance, effectiveness, value, and outcomes delivered by the Migration Assurance Platform.

These templates ensure:

* Consistent executive reporting
* Governance visibility
* Operational transparency
* Programme accountability
* Continuous improvement
* Value realisation tracking
* Investor and stakeholder reporting

---
