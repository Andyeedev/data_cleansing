# Executive Summary

## Purpose

The Pilot Customer Programme is designed to secure MAP's first reference customers, validate market demand, prove business value, and establish the evidence required for:

* Enterprise sales
* Microsoft ecosystem engagement
* Azure Marketplace readiness
* Investor discussions
* Strategic partnerships
* Future product development

The pilot programme is structured to minimise customer risk while maximising learning, value creation, and commercial opportunity.
