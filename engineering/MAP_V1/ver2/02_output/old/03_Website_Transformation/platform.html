Platform Overview

MAP (Migration Assurance Platform) is a purpose-built solution designed to manage the complexity of large-scale data and system migrations. Built on decades of financial services expertise, MAP provides the governance, validation, and reporting capabilities required for successful transformation programmes.

Our platform eliminates the guesswork from migrations by providing automated discovery, intelligent mapping, comprehensive validation, and real-time governance—all backed by an immutable evidence repository.

Core Platform Engines

Four powerful engines working in concert to deliver comprehensive migration assurance

Discovery Engine

Automated Intelligence

Automated identification and cataloguing of all migration assets, dependencies, and business rules across the enterprise landscape.

  • Smart Scanning: AI-powered discovery that learns from your environment and adapts scanning patterns
  • Dependency Graph: Visual mapping of system interdependencies with impact analysis
  • Schema Extraction: Automated database schema and business logic extraction
  • Change Detection: Continuous monitoring for environment changes during migration

Mapping Engine

Intelligent Transformation

Advanced field-level mapping with AI-assisted recommendations and comprehensive transformation rule management.

  • AI Recommendations: Machine learning-powered mapping suggestions based on historical patterns
  • Transformation Logic: Visual rule builder for complex data transformations
  • Version Control: Full audit trail of mapping changes with rollback capabilities
  • Collaboration: Multi-user editing with conflict resolution and approval workflows

Validation Engine

Comprehensive Verification

End-to-end validation framework ensuring data integrity and business rule compliance throughout the migration.

  • Test Automation: Configurable test suites with automated execution and reporting
  • Reconciliation: Record-level and aggregate reconciliation between systems
  • Exception Handling: Automated exception detection, classification, and routing
  • Performance Metrics: Real-time monitoring against defined SLAs and thresholds

Governance Engine

Policy Enforcement

Enterprise-grade governance framework ensuring compliance, risk management, and audit readiness throughout the programme.

  • Workflow Automation: Configurable approval workflows with SLA tracking
  • Risk Management: Centralised risk register with automated escalation
  • Compliance: Regulatory compliance monitoring with evidence collection
  • Access Control: Role-based permissions with segregation of duties
