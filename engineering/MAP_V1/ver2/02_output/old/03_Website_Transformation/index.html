Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, contact our support team.

How long does a typical migration project take?

Most enterprise migrations are completed within 8-12 weeks. MAP accelerates this timeline by automating discovery, validation, and reporting, reducing manual effort by up to 60%.

Can MAP handle complex data transformations?

Yes! MAP supports complex field mappings, data type conversions, business rule transformations, and custom validation logic. Our AI-powered engine suggests optimal transformations based on source and target schemas.

Is my data secure during migration?

Security is our top priority. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption, regular security audits, and are fully GDPR compliant. Our data centers are SOC 2 Type II certified with 99.9% uptime guarantee and daily backups.

Does MAP integrate with Azure services?

MAP is purpose-built for Azure with native integrations for Azure SQL, Cosmos DB, Data Lake, Synapse, and more. We also support hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

What kind of support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive support including dedicated migration architects, 24/7 technical support, comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and ongoing webinars. Enterprise customers receive a dedicated account manager.

Can I try MAP before committing?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all platform modules. No credit card required. We also provide personalized demos to show you how MAP can accelerate your specific migration project.
