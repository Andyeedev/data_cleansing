## 1. Purpose & Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This Sales Playbook provides a structured, repeatable approach for the MAP sales team to engage prospects, qualify opportunities, demonstrate value, and close deals. It serves as the single source of truth for all sales activities related to the Migration Assurance Platform.

### 1.2 Scope

This playbook covers:
- End-to-end sales process from lead qualification to deal close
- Consultative and solution selling methodologies
- Qualification frameworks (BANT, MEDDIC)
- Discovery, demo, objection handling, proposal, negotiation, and close processes
- Best practices for enterprise B2B technology sales

### 1.3 Audience

- Account Executives (AEs)
- Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)
- Solutions Engineers (SEs)
- Sales Managers and Directors
- Customer Success handoff participants

### 1.4 Document Dependencies

| Dependency | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| MAP Product Documentation | Technical specifications and features |
| MAP Pricing Guide | Current pricing models and tiers |
| MAP Case Studies | Customer success stories |
| MAP Competitive Analysis | Market positioning and differentiators |

## 2. Sales Methodology

### 2.1 Overview

MAP employs a hybrid sales methodology combining **Consultative Selling** and **Solution Selling**. This approach positions MAP as a trusted advisor that solves complex migration challenges rather than a transactional product vendor.

### 2.2 Consultative Selling Framework


### 2.3 Solution Selling Principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Customer-Centric** | Frame every conversation around customer outcomes, not product features |
| **Problem-First** | Understand the migration pain before prescribing MAP as the solution |
| **Value-Driven** | Quantify the business impact of migration risk reduction |
| **Stakeholder-Aligned** | Map and engage all decision makers and influencers |
| **Evidence-Based** | Use data, case studies, and ROI calculations to support claims |

## 3. Qualification Framework

### 3.1 BANT Framework

#### Budget

| Question | What to Listen For | Green Flag | Red Flag |
|----------|-------------------|------------|----------|
| What is your annual budget for migration initiatives? | Specific budget allocation | $500K+ allocated | No budget identified |
| When does your fiscal year start? | Budget cycle timing | Budget cycle aligns | Budget locked for 12+ months |
| Who controls the budget? | Budget authority identified | Direct budget owner | Multiple approval layers |
| Have you allocated funds for validation tooling? | Existing budget for tools | Line item for QA/validation | No tooling budget |

#### Authority

| Question | What to Listen For | Green Flag | Red Flag |
|----------|-------------------|------------|----------|
| Who will make the final decision on this purchase? | Decision maker identified | C-level or VP sponsor | No clear decision maker |
| Who else is involved in the evaluation? | Buying committee mapped | 3-5 stakeholders identified | 10+ stakeholders, unclear process |
| What is the approval process for purchases over $100K? | Procurement process known | Direct approval path | Complex procurement hurdles |
| Do you have a champion inside the organization? | Internal advocate exists | Active champion engaged | No internal champion |

#### Need

| Question | What to Listen For | Green Flag | Red Flag |
|----------|-------------------|------------|----------|
| What migration challenges are you currently facing? | Specific pain points articulated | Critical migration pain | No perceived urgency |
| What happens if you don't solve this problem? | Business impact quantified | High cost of inaction | Low business impact |
| What is your current migration success rate? | Metrics shared | Below 85% success rate | Already at 98%+ success |
| How are you currently validating migrations? | Current process described | Manual, error-prone process | Already has robust tooling |

#### Timeline

| Question | What to Listen For | Green Flag | Red Flag |
|----------|-------------------|------------|----------|
| What is your target go-live date? | Specific timeline | 3-6 month timeline | 12+ months out |
| What events are driving this timeline? | Business drivers identified | Regulatory deadline, merger | No specific driver |
| When do you need to make a decision? | Decision timeline known | Decision within 60 days | Decision 6+ months out |
| What happens if you miss this timeline? | Consequences understood | Significant business impact | Low impact of delay |

### 3.2 MEDDIC Framework

#### Metrics

| Element | Assessment Criteria | Scoring |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Business Metrics** | Quantifiable impact of migration risk | 1-5 (1=No metrics, 5=Clear metrics) |
| **Success Metrics** | How buyer defines success | 1-5 (1=Vague, 5=Specific) |
| **Cost Metrics** | Current cost of migration failures | 1-5 (1=Unknown, 5=Well-documented) |

| Element | Assessment Criteria | Scoring |

| **Identified** | Economic buyer named and confirmed | Yes/No |
| **Engaged** | Direct access to economic buyer | Yes/No |
| **Aligned** | Economic buyer agrees with value proposition | Yes/No |
| **Accessible** | Can reach economic buyer directly | Yes/No |

#### Decision Criteria

| Element | Assessment Criteria | Scoring |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Technical Criteria** | MAP meets technical requirements | 1-5 (1=Gap, 5=Full fit) |
| **Business Criteria** | MAP meets business requirements | 1-5 (1=Gap, 5=Full fit) |
| **Commercial Criteria** | MAP meets commercial requirements | 1-5 (1=Gap, 5=Full fit) |

#### Decision Process

| Element | Assessment Criteria | Scoring |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Process Mapped** | Buyer's decision process documented | Yes/No |
| **Steps Identified** | All decision steps known | Yes/No |
| **Timeline Confirmed** | Decision timeline validated | Yes/No |
| **Stakeholders Mapped** | All influencers identified | Yes/No |

#### Paper Process

| Element | Assessment Criteria | Scoring |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Legal Review** | Legal process understood | Yes/No |
| **Procurement** | Procurement process mapped | Yes/No |
| **Security Review** | Security requirements documented | Yes/No |
| **Contract Terms** | Standard terms acceptable | Yes/No |

#### Identify Pain

| Element | Assessment Criteria | Scoring |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Pain Identified** | Core migration pain documented | 1-5 (1=Minor, 5=Critical) |
| **Pain Quantified** | Business impact of pain measured | 1-5 (1=Unquantified, 5=Precisely measured) |
| **Pain Acknowledged** | Buyer agrees this is a priority | 1-5 (1=Disagree, 5=Urgent) |
| **Pain Sponsor** | Executive sponsor for solving pain | Yes/No |

### 3.3 Qualification Scoring

| Total Score | Qualification Level | Action |
|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| **80-100** | **Hot** | Priority opportunity, accelerate sales cycle |
| **60-79** | **Warm** | Continue qualification, develop champion |
| **40-59** | **Cool** | Nurture, address gaps, reassess in 30 days |
| **Below 40** | **Cold** | Disqualify or park, revisit in 90 days |

## 8. Negotiation Guide

### 8.1 Negotiation Principles

| Principle | Description | Application |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Value-Based** | Never negotiate on price alone; always tie to value | "At this price, MAP delivers 5x ROI" |
| **Principled** | Focus on interests, not positions | "What matters most to you: price or implementation?" |
| **Win-Win** | Both parties must feel good about the outcome | "Let's find a solution that works for both of us" |
| **Prepared** | Know your limits and alternatives before negotiating | Set clear walk-away points |

| Extended payment terms | Multi-year commitment | Budget constraints |
| Additional users | Higher tier purchase | Volume leverage |
| Implementation support | Faster close | Timeline pressure |
| Training days | Executive sponsor | Champion development |
| Custom feature | Strategic logo | Market entry |

### 8.4 Negotiation Tactics

#### Anchoring

```
"Based on the value MAP delivers, our standard pricing is $300,000 annually.
However, given your specific needs and commitment level, we can discuss
options starting at $250,000."
```

#### Silence

```
[After presenting price]
[Wait for prospect to respond]
[Do not fill silence]
[Let them process and respond first]
```

#### The Flinch

```
"I understand that's higher than expected. Let me see what we can do
to make this work within your budget."
[Pause, review options]
"Here's what I can offer..."
```

#### The Nibble

```
"We're close to agreement. If we can finalize this today, I can include
[small additional value] at no extra cost."
```

### 8.5 Common Negotiation Scenarios

| Scenario | Strategy | Response |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **"Your competitor is 30% cheaper"** | Value differentiation | "Let me show you why MAP delivers 3x more value" |
| **"We need a 50% discount"** | Phased approach | "Let's start with a smaller scope that fits your budget" |
| **"We'll sign today for 25% off"** | Hold firm on value | "I appreciate the urgency. Let me see what I can do" |
| **"We need net-90 payment terms"** | Trade for commitment | "We can do net-60 if you sign a 2-year agreement" |

## 10. Best Practices

### 10.1 Customer-Centric Practices

| Practice | Description | Implementation |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Listen First** | Spend 70% of discovery listening | Use open-ended questions |
| **Understand Before Prescribing** | Diagnose before recommending | Complete discovery before demo |
| **Speak Their Language** | Use customer terminology | Mirror their vocabulary |
| **Focus on Outcomes** | Talk results, not features | Lead with business value |
| **Build Relationships** | Invest in long-term partnerships | Regular check-ins beyond sales |

### 10.2 Value-Focused Practices

| Practice | Description | Implementation |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Quantify Everything** | Put numbers on pain and value | Use ROI calculator |
| **Tell Stories** | Use case studies and examples | Match industry and use case |
| **Show, Don't Tell** | Demo value, don't describe it | Customize demos to customer |
| **Third-Party Validation** | Use analyst reports and reviews | Reference Gartner, Forrester |
| **Competitive Differentiation** | Highlight unique advantages | Use competitive battle cards |

### 10.3 Structured Practices

| Practice | Description | Implementation |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Follow the Process** | Use defined sales stages | Update CRM regularly |
| **Document Everything** | Record all interactions | Use meeting notes template |
| **Qualify Rigorously** | Use BANT and MEDDIC | Score all opportunities |
| **Plan Every Meeting** | Set agendas and objectives | Send pre-reads in advance |
| **Track Metrics** | Monitor KPIs weekly | Review pipeline weekly |

### 10.4 Communication Best Practices

| Situation | Best Practice | Avoid |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| **Initial Outreach** | Personalized, value-focused | Generic templates |
| **Discovery Call** | Structured questions, active listening | Unprepared, shotgun approach |
| **Demo** | Story-driven, customer-specific | Feature dump, generic demo |
| **Proposal** | Executive summary first, visual | Long text-heavy documents |
| **Negotiation** | Win-win, value-based | Aggressive, adversarial |
| **Close** | Clear next steps, commitment | Vague, unclear expectations |
