## 1. Purpose

This document defines the Partner Strategy for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP). It establishes the framework for identifying, recruiting, enabling, managing, and growing a partner ecosystem that accelerates MAP's market penetration in the financial services migration sector. The strategy covers Systems Integrators (SIs), Independent Software Vendors (ISVs), Cloud Solution Providers (CSPs), and specialist consultancies, providing a structured approach to building mutually beneficial partnerships.

### 1.1 Scope

This strategy covers:

- Partner identification, classification, and segmentation
- Partner programme tiers, benefits, and requirements
- Partner enablement through training, certification, and support
- Partner sales models including co-sell, referral, and reseller arrangements
- Joint marketing activities, campaigns, and event participation
- Partner onboarding, development, governance, and performance management
- Legal frameworks, commercial terms, and conflict resolution

### 1.2 Audience

| Role                          | Purpose                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| MAP Executive Leadership      | Strategic investment and partnership decisions  |
| MAP Business Development      | Partner recruitment and relationship management|
| MAP Engineering               | Technical enablement and integration support   |
| MAP Marketing                 | Joint GTM activities and co-marketing          |
| MAP Legal                     | Contract negotiation and compliance             |
| Prospective Partners          | Understanding MAP partner programme            |

## 6. Partner Sales Model

### 6.1 Sales Models

| Model          | Description                                    | Revenue Share | MAP Effort | Partner Effort |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ---------- | -------------- |
| Co-Sell        | MAP and partner sell jointly to customer       | 80/20 (MAP/partner) | High  | High           |
| Referral       | Partner refers customer, MAP closes            | 10% referral fee | Low    | Low            |
| Resell         | Partner sells MAP subscription to customer     | 20-30% margin | Low       | High           |
| Implement      | Partner implements MAP for customer             | 100% to partner | Low      | High           |
| Managed Service| Partner manages MAP deployment for customer    | 15-25% ongoing | Low      | High           |

### 6.2 Deal Registration Process

| Step | Action                           | Owner           | SLA       |
| ---- | -------------------------------- | --------------- | --------- |
| 1    | Partner identifies opportunity   | Partner         | Ongoing   |
| 2    | Partner submits deal registration| Partner         | Same day  |
| 3    | MAP validates registration       | MAP BD          | 48 hours  |
| 4    | MAP approves/assigns             | MAP BD          | 48 hours  |
| 5    | Joint pursuit begins             | MAP + Partner   | Per plan  |
| 6    | Technical validation             | MAP Engineering | 5 days    |
| 7    | Commercial proposal              | MAP Sales       | 5 days    |
| 8    | Deal closing                     | MAP Sales       | Per cycle |
| 9    | Commission/payment processing    | MAP Finance     | 30 days   |

### 6.3 Commission Structure

| Sales Model      | Commission Rate | Payment Timeline | Conditions                        |
| ---------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Referral         | 10%             | Net 30           | Customer closes within 90 days    |
| Co-Sell (MAP-led)| 5-10%           | Net 30           | Partner contributes to deal       |
| Co-Sell (Partner-led)| 15-20%      | Net 30           | MAP contributes to deal           |
| Resell           | 20-30%          | Net 30           | Partner manages full sales cycle  |
| Managed Service  | 15-25%          | Monthly          | Ongoing service delivery          |
| Implementation   | 100%            | Per SOW          | Partner delivers implementation   |

### 6.4 Conflict Resolution

| Conflict Type                    | Resolution Approach                               |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Duplicate deal registration      | First-to-register wins, unless compelling reason   |
| MAP direct vs. partner deal      | Partner gets priority if registered first          |
| Multi-partner competing          | Best-fit partner based on capability and relationship |
| Territory overlap                | Primary territory partner gets preference          |
| Commercial disagreement          | Escalation to MAP BD Director + Partner Director   |
