## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Purpose

This Customer Acquisition Strategy defines the end-to-end process for acquiring, converting, onboarding, and retaining customers for MAP (Migration Assurance Platform). It provides a systematic approach to filling the pipeline, converting prospects to paying customers, and ensuring long-term success.

### 1.2 Vision

To build a predictable, scalable customer acquisition engine that delivers consistent growth while maximizing customer lifetime value and minimizing churn.

### 4.2 Lead Classification

| Total Score | Classification | SLA | Action |
|-------------|---------------|-----|--------|
| **80-100** | **Hot Lead** | 4 hours | Immediate sales follow-up |
| **60-79** | **Warm Lead** | 24 hours | Sales qualification |
| **40-59** | **Nurture Lead** | 1 week | Marketing nurture sequence |
| **20-39** | **Cold Lead** | 1 month | Re-engagement campaign |
| **Below 20** | **Disqualified** | N/A | Remove from active list |

### 4.3 Lead Scoring Implementation

#### Technology Requirements

| Component | Purpose | Integration |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Marketing Automation** | Track behaviors, assign scores | Marketo, HubSpot |
| **CRM** | Store lead data, track conversions | Salesforce |
| **Analytics** | Measure scoring accuracy | Google Analytics |
| **Enrichment** | Add firmographic data | Clearbit, ZoomInfo |
| **Intent Data** | Track buying signals | Bombora, G2 |

#### Scoring Rules

```
Rule 1: If Lead.FitScore >= 80 AND Lead.InterestScore >= 60
        Then Lead.Classification = "Hot Lead"

Rule 2: If Lead.FitScore >= 60 AND Lead.InterestScore >= 40
        Then Lead.Classification = "Warm Lead"

Rule 3: If Lead.FitScore >= 40 OR Lead.InterestScore >= 40
        Then Lead.Classification = "Nurture Lead"

Rule 4: If Lead.FitScore < 40 AND Lead.InterestScore < 40
        Then Lead.Classification = "Cold Lead"
```

#### Health Score Calculation

```
Health Score = (Usage * 0.30) + (Features * 0.20) + (Support * 0.15) 
             + (NPS * 0.15) + (Engagement * 0.10) + (Payment * 0.10)

Green: Score >= 80
Yellow: Score 60-79
Red: Score < 60
```
