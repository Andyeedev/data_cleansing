## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Document Purpose

This document defines the comprehensive Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP). It provides a structured framework for launching MAP into the UK Financial Services market and subsequent geographic expansion. This strategy serves as the authoritative reference for all launch activities, revenue targets, channel decisions, and market positioning.

### 1.2 Scope

This GTM strategy covers:

- Market entry approach and sequencing
- Launch phase definitions and gates
- Revenue model and pricing framework
- Channel strategy and partner ecosystem
- Geographic expansion roadmap
- Marketing and sales alignment
- Customer success and retention
- 12-month execution roadmap
- Success metrics and KPIs

### 1.3 Audience

| Audience | Use |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Strategic direction, investment decisions |
| Product Management | Feature prioritization, launch readiness |
| Sales & Marketing | Campaign planning, pipeline development |
| Customer Success | Onboarding, retention strategies |

## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1 Overview

MAP is positioned to capture the rapidly growing Financial Services data migration validation market, valued at £2.4B in the UK and growing at 18% CAGR. Financial institutions face unprecedented regulatory pressure to ensure data integrity during cloud migrations, creating a critical need for automated validation and compliance assurance.

### 2.2 Key Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Regulatory-First Positioning** | Lead with compliance and audit-readiness as primary value proposition |
| **Phased Geographic Expansion** | UK → EU → Global, building credibility and case studies at each stage |
| **Multi-Channel Distribution** | Direct sales for enterprise, partner channel for mid-market, marketplace for SMB |
| **Outcome-Based Selling** | Price based on migration value protected, not seats or usage |
| **Ecosystem Integration** | Deep integrations with major cloud platforms and SI partners |

## 5. Launch Phases

### 5.1 Phase Overview

MAP follows a three-phase launch model designed to validate market demand, refine product-market fit, and scale sustainably.

```
Phase 1: Preview    Phase 2: Beta     Phase 3: GA
(Months 1-3)       (Months 4-6)      (Months 7-12)
┌──────────────┐    ┌──────────────┐   ┌──────────────┐
│ Design       │    │ Closed Beta  │   │ General      │
│ Partners     │───▶│ Early        │──▶│ Availability │
│ 3-5 accounts │    │ Adopters     │   │ Full Launch  │
│ Free access  │    │ 10-15 accts  │   │ 30+ customers│
└──────────────┘    └──────────────┘   └──────────────┘
```
