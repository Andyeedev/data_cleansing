## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Objective

This document defines the comprehensive communications strategy for the launch of MAP (Migration Assurance Platform), an enterprise-grade solution designed to validate, automate, and govern financial services data migrations. The plan ensures coordinated, consistent, and measurable communications across all stakeholder groups from pre-launch through post-launch phases.

### 1.2 Scope

The communications plan covers all external and internal messaging, channel selection, content creation, timeline management, and performance measurement for the MAP product launch. It integrates with the overall go-to-market strategy and aligns with brand positioning, sales enablement, and customer success objectives.

### 1.3 Key Outcomes

| Outcome | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Market Awareness | Establish MAP as the definitive platform for financial services migration validation |
| Pipeline Generation | Drive qualified leads into the sales funnel within the first 90 days |
| Customer Adoption | Achieve first 10 enterprise customers within 6 months of launch |
| Brand Positioning | Position the company as a thought leader in migration assurance |
| Internal Alignment | Ensure all internal teams are informed, equipped, and aligned on messaging |

### 1.4 Document Structure

This plan is organized into communication objectives, audience definitions, channel strategies, timeline phases, content plans, templates, metrics, and best practices. Each section includes actionable items, responsible owners, and measurable outcomes.
