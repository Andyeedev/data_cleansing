## 1. Overview

| Objective | Description | Target |

## 8. Success Criteria

### Overall Success Criteria

The AI adoption initiative is considered successful when:

1. **Productivity:** 40%+ time savings per feature
2. **Quality:** 50%+ defect rate reduction
3. **Adoption:** 90%+ active users across engineering
4. **Satisfaction:** 80%+ developer satisfaction score
5. **ROI:** Positive ROI within 6 months
6. **Prompt Library:** 100+ active prompts with 60%+ reuse rate
7. **Compliance:** 100% governance compliance
8. **Innovation:** 3+ advanced AI capabilities adopted
