## 1. Overview

This document defines the skill progression framework for engineers working with AI-assisted development on the MAP platform.

---

## 2. Level 1: Beginner (1-2 months)

### Skills

- Understanding AI capabilities and limitations in software development
- Basic prompt writing for code generation and explanation
- Using AI for code completion and suggestion
- Reviewing AI-generated code for correctness and quality

### Certification Criteria

- Pass AI fundamentals assessment (80% minimum)
- Demonstrate 5 effective AI prompts in code review
- Complete 3 AI-assisted coding exercises
- Submit 2 prompts to team prompt library

---

## 3. Level 2: Intermediate (3-6 months)

### Skills

- Advanced prompt engineering with context management
- Using AI for test generation and edge case identification
- AI-assisted debugging and root cause analysis
- Creating custom prompts for MAP-specific patterns
- Evaluating AI output quality and identifying hallucinations

### Certification Criteria

- Pass advanced prompt engineering assessment
- Create 3 MAP-specific prompt templates accepted into library
- Successfully use AI to identify and fix 5 production issues
- Mentor at least 1 Level 1 engineer

---

## 4. Level 3: Advanced (6-12 months)

### Skills

- AI architecture integration into development workflows
- Prompt library management and optimization
- AI workflow automation and pipeline integration
- Training team members on AI best practices
- Evaluating AI tool effectiveness and ROI

### Certification Criteria

- Design and deploy 3 AI workflow automations
- Lead AI training session for engineering team
- Achieve measurable improvement in team AI adoption metrics
- Publish internal case study on AI workflow optimization

---

## 5. Level 4: Expert (12-24 months)

### Skills

- AI strategy development for development organization
- Custom AI tool integration and API development
- AI governance implementation and policy definition
- Measuring AI ROI across development lifecycle
- Cross-team AI adoption and standardization

### Certification Criteria

- Develop and implement AI strategy document
- Achieve measurable ROI improvement through AI initiatives
- Lead successful AI tool adoption across 2+ teams
- Publish internal whitepaper on AI best practices

---

## 6. Level 5: AI Architect (24-36 months)

### Skills

- Enterprise AI strategy and roadmap development
- AI platform selection and architecture design
- AI governance framework and policy development
- AI culture transformation and organizational change
- AI ethics and responsible AI implementation

### Certification Criteria

- Develop enterprise AI strategy approved by executive leadership
- Implement AI governance framework with measurable outcomes
- Complete executive AI leadership program
- Lead successful AI transformation initiative

---

## 7. Progression Path

```
Level 1: Beginner (1-2 months)
    |
Level 2: Intermediate (3-6 months)
    |
Level 3: Advanced (6-12 months)
    |
Level 4: Expert (12-24 months)
    |
Level 5: AI Architect (24-36 months)
```

**Total progression time: 3-6 years for full AI Architect mastery**
