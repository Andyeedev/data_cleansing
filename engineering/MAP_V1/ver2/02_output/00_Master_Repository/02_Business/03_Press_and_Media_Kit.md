## 7. Boilerplate

### Short (25 words)

### Medium (50 words)
MAP (Migration Assurance Platform) is an Azure-first SaaS platform that validates, governs, and assures enterprise cloud migrations before production deployment. We provide the assurance layer that ensures every migration is validated, governed, and assured before it goes live.

### Long (100 words)
MAP (Migration Assurance Platform) is an Azure-first SaaS platform that validates, governs, and assures enterprise cloud migrations before production deployment. Organisations lose data, suffer downtime, and face compliance because migrations lack proper validation. MAP provides the assurance layer between migration planning and go-live — ensuring every migration is validated, governed, and assured before it goes live. Our platform automates validation, provides governance dashboards, and delivers executive visibility — giving enterprises confidence that their migrations will succeed.
