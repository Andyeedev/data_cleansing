## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Document Purpose

This document establishes the Commercial Model for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) Pilot Deployment. It defines the pricing structures, licensing options, financial projections, and commercial frameworks required to successfully execute pilot programmes and convert them to paid production deployments. This model ensures sustainable growth while providing value to pilot customers.

### 1.2 Framework Objectives

- **Revenue Protection:** Ensure pilot investments are recoverable through conversion
- **Customer Value:** Provide clear pricing that reflects the value delivered
- **Market Competitiveness:** Position pricing competitively within the migration platform market
- **Scalability:** Design pricing models that support customer growth
- **Predictability:** Create forecastable revenue streams and financial projections
- **Flexibility:** Offer multiple pricing options to suit different customer needs
- **Transparency:** Clear terms and conditions for all commercial arrangements

### 1.3 Applicability

This commercial model applies to all MAP Pilot Deployment activities:

| Commercial Element | Scope | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot Duration | Standard and extended pilot terms | Critical |
| Free Pilot Criteria | Eligibility for complimentary pilots | High |
| Pricing Models | User-based, usage-based, tiered pricing | Critical |
| Conversion Criteria | Success metrics for pilot-to-paid conversion | Critical |
| Licensing | Licence types, terms, and conditions | Critical |
| Expansion | Upsell and cross-sell opportunities | High |
| Financial Projections | Revenue, cost, and margin forecasts | High |
