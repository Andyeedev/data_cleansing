## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Document Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive analysis of the target market for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP). It defines market segmentation, sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), target industries, company profiles, decision makers, and buying triggers. This analysis serves as the foundation for sales targeting, marketing campaigns, and product positioning.

### 1.2 Scope

This target market analysis covers:

- Market segmentation by industry, size, and geography
- Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- Target industry deep-dives
- Company size and profile definitions
- Decision maker and buying committee mapping
- Buying trigger identification and analysis
- Market dynamics and trends
- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) definition
- Account scoring framework

### 1.3 Audience

| Audience | Use |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Investment decisions, market prioritisation |
| Sales Team | Account targeting, territory planning |
| Marketing Team | Campaign targeting, content strategy |
| Product Management | Feature prioritisation, market needs |
| Business Development | Partnership targeting, channel strategy |

### 1.4 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market — total market demand for a product or service |
| **SAM** | Serviceable Addressable Market — portion of TAM that can be served |
| **SOM** | Serviceable Obtainable Market — portion of SAM that can be captured |
| **ICP** | Ideal Customer Profile — description of the perfect customer |
| **Buying Committee** | Group of stakeholders involved in a purchase decision |
| **Buying Trigger** | Event or condition that initiates a buying process |
| **ACV** | Annual Contract Value |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue |

## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1 Market Opportunity Overview

The Financial Services data migration validation market represents a significant and growing opportunity. Driven by cloud migration mandates, regulatory requirements, and digital transformation initiatives, UK Financial Services institutions are actively seeking solutions to ensure data integrity during migration.

### 2.2 Key Findings

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Market Size** | UK FS data migration market: £480M (2026), growing at 18% CAGR |
| **Target Segment** | UK Financial Services firms with 500-5,000 employees |
| **Primary Industries** | Banking, Insurance, Asset Management, Fintech |
| **Key Buyers** | CIO, CTO, Head of Migration, Enterprise Architect |
| **Buying Triggers** | Cloud migration, regulatory changes, cost pressure |
| **Competitive Gap** | No purpose-built FS migration validation platform exists |

### 2.3 Market Sizing Summary

| Market | Value (2026) | Growth | MAP Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | £2.4B | 15% | UK Financial Services IT |
| **SAM** | £480M | 18% | FS data migration & validation |

TAM represents the total market demand for data migration and validation solutions across all Financial Services organisations globally.

#### 4.1.2 TAM Calculation
