## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Document Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive messaging framework for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP). It establishes message hierarchies, audience-specific messaging, feature-to-benefit mappings, elevator pitches, taglines, and content templates to ensure consistent, effective communication across all channels and audiences.

### 1.2 Objectives

- Establish a clear message hierarchy for all MAP communications
- Create audience-specific messaging for CIO, Technical, and Business personas
- Map features to benefits with supporting proof points
- Develop elevator pitches for various time constraints
- Define tagline options and usage guidelines
- Provide content templates for blog, whitepaper, and case study formats

### 1.3 Audience for This Document

| Role | Usage |
|------|-------|
| Product Marketing | Primary messaging owner |
| Content Team | Content creation and templates |
| Sales Team | Customer conversations and proposals |
| Marketing Team | Campaign and collateral creation |
| Executive Team | Investor and partner communications |

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In Scope

- Message hierarchy (headline, subheadline, body, CTA)
- Audience-specific messaging (CIO, Technical, Business)
- Feature-to-benefit mapping with proof points
- Elevator pitches (30-second, 60-second, 2-minute)
- Tagline options and usage guidelines
- Content templates (blog, whitepaper, case study)
- Messaging best practices and guidelines

### 2.2 Out of Scope
