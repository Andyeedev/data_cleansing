## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Document Purpose

This document defines five detailed customer personas for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP). Each persona represents a key stakeholder in the Financial Services data migration buying process. These personas inform product development, marketing messaging, sales enablement, and customer success strategies.

### 1.2 Scope

This document covers:

- Persona development methodology
- Five detailed persona profiles with demographics, goals, pain points, and behaviours
- Empathy maps for each persona
- Customer journey maps
- Persona-based messaging frameworks
- Sales playbooks for each persona
- Validation methodology

### 1.3 Audience

| Audience | Use |
|---|---|
| Product Management | Feature prioritisation, UX design |
| Marketing | Messaging, content creation, campaigns |
| Sales | Sales enablement, objection handling |
| Customer Success | Onboarding, engagement strategies |
| Executive Leadership | Customer understanding, strategy |

### 1.4 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Persona** | Fictional character representing a user archetype |
| **Empathy Map** | Visual tool to capture persona attitudes and behaviours |
| **Journey Map** | Visual representation of persona experience over time |
| **Buying Committee** | Group of stakeholders involved in purchase decision |
| **Champion** | Internal advocate for MAP within customer organisation |

## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1 Persona Overview

MAP targets five key personas in Financial Services organisations. Each persona plays a distinct role in the migration validation buying process, with different priorities, pain points, and decision criteria.

| # | Persona | Role | Primary Focus | MAP Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CIO | Technology Strategy | ROI, Risk, Business Alignment | Economic Buyer |
| 2 | Head of Migration | Programme Delivery | Delivery, Quality, Timeline | Champion |
| 3 | Enterprise Architect | Architecture | Integration, Standards, Scalability | Technical Evaluator |
| 4 | QA Lead | Quality Assurance | Quality, Compliance, Testing | User Buyer |
| 5 | CFO | Financial Management | Cost, ROI, Value | Economic Buyer |

### 2.2 Key Insights

| Insight | Implication |
|---|---|
| CIOs prioritise business outcomes over technology | Lead with ROI and business value |
| Head of Migration is the most likely champion | Invest in this relationship early |
| Enterprise Architects need technical validation | Provide architecture documentation |
| QA Leads need compliance assurance | Emphasise audit trails and testing |
| CFOs need financial justification | Provide clear TCO and ROI models |

### 2.3 Persona Usage

| Use Case | Application |
|---|---|
| Product Development | Feature prioritisation based on persona needs |
| Marketing | Persona-specific messaging and content |
| Sales | Persona-based sales approach and objection handling |
| Customer Success | Personalised onboarding and engagement |
| Customer Support | Persona-appropriate communication style |
