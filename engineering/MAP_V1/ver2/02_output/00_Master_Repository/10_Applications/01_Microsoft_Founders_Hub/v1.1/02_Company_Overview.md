## Why MAP Exists

Enterprise cloud migrations represent one of the highest-risk activities organisations undertake. Despite significant investment, migrations continue to fail — not because of tooling limitations, but because of a systemic gap between migration planning and production deployment.

Edward Odewale witnessed this pattern repeatedly across enterprise migration programmes in financial services and regulated industries: data loss, compliance exposure, unplanned downtime, and governance failures that could have been prevented with proper validation before go-live. Existing migration tools focus on moving workloads. Almost none focus on validating them.

MAP was created to close this gap — providing the validation, governance, and assurance layer that enterprises require before every migration reaches production. MAP exists to give organisations confidence that their migrations are ready before they go live.

## Target Industries

MAP is purpose-built for industries where migration failure carries significant regulatory, operational, and financial risk:

- **Financial Services** — banks, insurers, asset managers, and payment providers operating under FCA, PRA, and Basel requirements.
- **Government** — public sector organisations modernising legacy systems under GDS and central government standards.
- **Insurance** — carriers and brokers managing complex policy and claims data migrations.
- **Healthcare** — providers and trusts migrating sensitive patient data under NHS Digital and DCB standards.
- **Regulated Industries** — any sector where migration failure triggers audit, compliance, or operational risk exposure.

## What Makes MAP Different

**Validation-First Approach**
MAP is designed to validate before deployment, not after. Every migration passes through a structured assurance process before reaching production.

**Azure-Native Architecture**
Built on Microsoft Azure from day one. MAP integrates natively with Entra ID, Azure SQL, Azure Storage, and Azure Monitor — ensuring seamless enterprise adoption within existing Azure estates.

**Executive Visibility**
MAP provides clear, actionable visibility into migration readiness — enabling confident go/no-go decisions at every stage.
