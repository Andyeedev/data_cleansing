## 1. Purpose & Scope

### 1.1 Document Purpose

This Risk Register provides a comprehensive identification, assessment, and mitigation framework for all risks associated with the MAP (Migration Assurance Platform) Production Readiness and Go-to-Market (GTM) initiative. It serves as the single source of truth for risk management across the 12-month GTM roadmap.

### 1.2 Scope

This register covers risks across four categories:

- **Technical Risks**: Platform performance, security, integration, and reliability
- **Commercial Risks**: Pricing, competition, market positioning, and revenue
- **Operational Risks**: Support, staffing, process, and vendor management
- **Market Risks**: Demand, timing, economy, and regulatory changes

### 1.3 Objectives

| Objective | Description |
|---|---|
| Identify | Comprehensive identification of all material risks |
| Assess | Systematic evaluation of probability and impact |
| Mitigate | Define actionable response strategies for each risk |
| Monitor | Ongoing tracking and reporting of risk status |
| Govern | Clear ownership, accountability, and escalation paths |

### 1.4 Intended Audience

| Role | Use |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Strategic risk oversight and investment decisions |
| Risk Owners | Day-to-day risk management and mitigation execution |
| Project Managers | Risk tracking within project workstreams |
| Department Heads | Departmental risk identification and reporting |
| Board of Directors | Enterprise risk oversight and governance |

### 1.5 Document Governance

This Risk Register is reviewed bi-weekly by the Risk Management Committee and updated monthly. Material changes require approval from the Chief Risk Officer. All risk owners are accountable for keeping their assigned risks current.

## 10. Risk Response Strategies

### 10.1 Response Strategy Definitions

| Strategy | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Avoid** | Eliminate the threat by removing the risk cause | High probability, high impact risks where elimination is feasible |
| **Mitigate** | Reduce probability or impact to acceptable levels | Risks where reduction is possible and cost-effective |
| **Transfer** | Shift the risk to a third party (insurance, outsourcing) | Risks where third party can better manage the risk |
| **Accept** | Acknowledge the risk and prepare contingency response | Low probability/impact risks or when mitigation cost exceeds benefit |
