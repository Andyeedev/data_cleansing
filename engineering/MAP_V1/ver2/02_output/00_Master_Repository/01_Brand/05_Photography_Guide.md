## 1. Style Categories

| Category | Description | Use Case |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Professional | Enterprise, corporate | Leadership, about |
| Cloud | Data centres, infrastructure | Technical pages |
| Consulting | Teams, collaboration | Solutions pages |
| Operations | Control rooms, dashboards | Product pages |
| Boardrooms | Executive meetings | Enterprise pages |

---

## 2. Avoid

- Stock photo clichés (handshakes, lightbulbs)
- Overly staged scenarios
- Casual/playful imagery
- Low-quality images

---

## 3. Colour Treatment

- Desaturated tones preferred
- Blue overlay acceptable
- Maintain brand colour palette
