| careers@mapnexus.com | Job applications | HR |

| founder@mapnexus.com | Founder direct | Founder |
| admin@mapnexus.com | Administration | Operations |
| noreply@mapnexus.com | Automated notifications | System |

### Naming Conventions

- Lowercase only
- No special characters
- Use dots for compound names (e.g., uk@mapnexus.com)
- Use hyphens for departments (e.g., uk-sales@mapnexus.com)
