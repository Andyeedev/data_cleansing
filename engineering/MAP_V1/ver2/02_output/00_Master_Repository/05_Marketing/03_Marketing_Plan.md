## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Purpose

This Marketing Plan defines the comprehensive go-to-market strategy for MAP (Migration Assurance Platform). It outlines the marketing mix, channel strategy, content roadmap, budget allocation, and performance metrics to drive demand generation, brand awareness, and revenue growth.

### 1.2 Marketing Vision

To establish MAP as the definitive market leader in migration assurance, becoming the trusted partner for financial services organizations undertaking critical data migration initiatives.

### 1.3 Marketing Mission

To educate, engage, and convert financial services technology leaders by demonstrating measurable value in migration risk reduction, data integrity assurance, and compliance enablement.

### 1.4 Key Objectives (FY2027)

| Objective | Target | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Brand Awareness** | 50% aided awareness in target segment | Brand survey |
| **Lead Generation** | 5,000 marketing qualified leads (MQLs) | Marketing automation |
| **Pipeline Contribution** | $50M marketing-sourced pipeline | CRM |
| **Revenue Influence** | $20M marketing-influenced revenue | Attribution model |
| **Content Engagement** | 500,000 content downloads | Analytics platform |
| **Event Attendance** | 2,000 event attendees | Event management |
| **Analyst Recognition** | Positioning in Gartner Magic Quadrant | Analyst relations |
