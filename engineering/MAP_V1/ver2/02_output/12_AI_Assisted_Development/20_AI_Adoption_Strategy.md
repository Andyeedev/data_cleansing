## 1. Overview

This document defines the phased adoption strategy for AI-assisted development across the MAP (Migration Assurance Platform) engineering organization. The strategy outlines four phases from pilot to continuous optimization, with clear entry/exit criteria, activities, success metrics, risks, and resource requirements for each phase.

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives

| Objective | Description | Target |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Productivity Enhancement | Reduce development time through AI assistance | 40% time savings |
| Quality Improvement | Maintain or improve code quality with AI | 50% defect reduction |
| Knowledge Democratization | Make expert knowledge accessible via AI prompts | 60% prompt reuse |
| Developer Experience | Improve developer satisfaction and reduce toil | 80%+ satisfaction |
| Competitive Advantage | Accelerate feature delivery to market | 2x feature velocity |

## 10. Success Criteria

### 10.1 Phase Success Criteria

| Phase | Primary Success Criteria | Secondary Success Criteria |
|-------|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| Pilot | Engineers comfortable with AI tools | Initial metrics collected, lessons documented |
| Team Adoption | Consistent usage across team | Measurable productivity gains |
| Enterprise | Enterprise-wide adoption | Governance compliance |
| Continuous | Continuous improvement | ROI maximization |

### 10.2 Overall Success Criteria

The AI adoption initiative is considered successful when:

1. **Productivity:** 40%+ time savings per feature
2. **Quality:** 50%+ defect rate reduction
3. **Adoption:** 90%+ active users across engineering
4. **Satisfaction:** 80%+ developer satisfaction score
5. **ROI:** Positive ROI within 6 months
6. **Prompt Library:** 100+ active prompts with 60%+ reuse rate
7. **Compliance:** 100% governance compliance
8. **Innovation:** 3+ advanced AI capabilities adopted

### 10.3 Failure Criteria

The initiative should be re-evaluated if:

1. Adoption rate below 50% after 3 months
2. Quality metrics show significant degradation
3. Developer satisfaction below 60%
4. ROI not achieved within 12 months
5. Security or compliance incidents occur
