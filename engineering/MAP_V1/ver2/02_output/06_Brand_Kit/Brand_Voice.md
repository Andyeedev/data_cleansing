# MAP Brand Voice

**Document:** Writing Style Guide
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** June 2026

---

## 1. Voice

**Professional. Clear. Evidence-based.**

---

## 2. Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Website | Confident, clear |
| Documentation | Precise, helpful |
| Marketing | Evidence-based, not hype |
| Support | Empathetic, solution-focused |
| Technical | Accurate, detailed |

---

## 3. Grammar Rules

- Use active voice
- Keep sentences short
- Avoid jargon when possible
- Define technical terms

---

## 4. Headlines

- Clear, not clever
- Benefit-focused
- Evidence-based
- Avoid hype words

---

## 5. Avoid

- Superlatives without evidence
- Exclamation marks (professional context)
- Jargon overload
- Marketing fluff
- Vague claims

---

*End of Brand Voice*
