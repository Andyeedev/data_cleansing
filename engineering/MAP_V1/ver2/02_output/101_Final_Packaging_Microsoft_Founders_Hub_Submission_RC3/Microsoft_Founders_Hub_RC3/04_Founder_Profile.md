# 04 — Founder Profile

**MAP — Migration Assurance Platform Ltd**
**Microsoft Founders Hub Application v1.1**

## Biography

Edward Odewale is the founder of MAP (Migration Assurance Platform), an enterprise software company specialising in cloud migration governance and assurance.

With 15+ years of experience in enterprise technology — spanning cloud architecture, data governance, and enterprise software in financial services and regulated industries — Edward identified a critical gap in the migration lifecycle: the lack of validation and governance before production deployment.

MAP was created to solve this problem. The platform provides the assurance layer between migration planning and go-live — ensuring every migration is validated, governed, and assured before it goes live. Edward believes that every migration should be validated and every outcome should be assured. MAP is the checkpoint that gives enterprises confidence in their cloud migrations.

## Founder-Market Fit

Edward's 15+ years in enterprise technology gave him direct exposure to the migration governance gap. Working across cloud architecture, data governance, and enterprise software in financial services and regulated industries, he witnessed repeated migration failures — not caused by inadequate tooling, but by the absence of structured validation before production deployment.

This experience is the foundation of MAP. Edward built the platform he saw the market needed: an assurance layer that validates, governs, and confirms migration readiness before go-live. His deep understanding of enterprise requirements, regulatory expectations, and Azure-native architecture ensures MAP is purpose-built for the industries where migration failure carries the highest cost.
