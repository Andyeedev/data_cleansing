# 03 — Product Overview

**MAP Nexus™ — Migration Assurance Platform**
**Microsoft Founders Hub Application v1.1**

---

## The Customer Problem

Enterprise cloud migrations fail at alarming rates. Organisations lose data, suffer unplanned downtime, and face compliance penalties because migrations lack proper validation before production deployment.

Existing tooling focuses on moving data. MAP focuses on validating it.

---

## The MAP Solution

MAP Nexus™ is a pilot-ready enterprise assurance platform that sits between migration planning and production deployment. It is designed to validate, govern, and assure migrations before go-live — reducing risk, accelerating delivery, and providing executives with the visibility they need.

---

## Core Capabilities

**Discovery**
Automated assessment of source and target environments to understand migration scope, dependencies, and readiness.

**Validation**
Structured validation controls designed to verify migration integrity, data accuracy, and deployment readiness before production.

**Governance**
Approval workflows, audit trails, and compliance evidence designed to satisfy enterprise governance and regulatory requirements.

**Reporting**
Executive dashboards and migration readiness reports that provide clear, actionable visibility into migration status.

**AI Assistance** *(future)*
AI-powered insights designed to accelerate validation, identify risk, and recommend corrective actions.

---

## Customer Outcomes

- **Reduced migration risk** — migrations validated before go-live, not remediated after.
- **Executive confidence** — clear visibility into readiness at every stage.
- **Compliance assurance** — audit-ready governance evidence for regulators and stakeholders.
- **Faster validation** — accelerated migration programmes with automated controls.

---

## Business Benefits

- Lower total cost of migration through early defect detection.
- Fewer failed deployments and unplanned downtime.
- Audit-ready reporting for regulators and stakeholders.
- Reduced manual validation effort across migration programmes.

---

## Supported Scenarios

- Database migrations

- Legacy platform modernisation
- Multi-system transformation programmes
