# 01 — Executive Summary

**MAP Nexus™ — Migration Assurance Platform**
**Microsoft Founders Hub Application**

---

## Company

**Migration Assurance Platform Ltd** (proposed)
Brand: **MAP**
Product: **MAP Nexus™**
Domain: mapnexus.com

---

## Mission

To eliminate migration failures through automated validation, governance, and continuous assurance — giving enterprises confidence before every go-live.

---

## Vision

To become the global standard for migration assurance — the trusted checkpoint that every enterprise migration must pass through.

---

## The Problem

Enterprise cloud migrations fail at alarming rates. Organisations across financial services and regulated industries face:

- **Data loss** — incomplete or inaccurate migrations compromise critical business data.
- **Unplanned downtime** — failed deployments disrupt operations and erode stakeholder confidence.
- **Compliance exposure** — audit gaps and governance failures invite regulatory scrutiny.
- **Cost overruns** — remediation of migration defects consumes budget and delays programmes.

Current tooling focuses on moving data. Almost nothing focuses on validating it before it goes live.

---

## The Solution

MAP Nexus™ provides an enterprise assurance layer that sits between migration planning and production deployment. It validates, governs, and assures every migration before go-live — reducing risk, accelerating delivery, and giving executives the visibility they need.

---

## Market Opportunity

The enterprise cloud migration market is projected to exceed **$100 billion by 2027**. UK financial services institutions alone manage thousands of active migration programmes, each requiring validation, governance, and regulatory assurance. MAP targets this market with a differentiated, Azure-first platform.

---

## Business Model

MAP operates on a **SaaS subscription model** supported by professional services including implementation, advisory, and managed validation engagements.

---

## Current Stage

MAP is **MVP ready** following seven completed development sprints with a **seven-person team**. The platform is Azure-native, built on Microsoft Entra ID, Azure SQL, Azure Storage, and Azure Monitor. MAP is seeking partnership through the Microsoft Founders Hub to accelerate commercialisation.

---

## Future Direction

MAP's roadmap includes AI-powered validation capabilities, multi-cloud support, and enterprise marketplace distribution — positioning the platform as the definitive standard for migration assurance across global markets.
