## 1. Company Description

Migration Assurance Platform Ltd is an enterprise SaaS company delivering MAP Nexus™, an Azure-first platform that validates, governs, and assures enterprise cloud migrations. We help financial services organisations migrate complex legacy systems to the cloud with confidence, eliminating the risk of data loss, compliance failure, and business disruption.

---

## 2. What Problem Are You Solving?

Enterprise cloud migrations fail at an alarming rate — up to 70% according to industry research — with financial services firms facing unique regulatory and operational risk. Organisations lack a unified, auditable assurance layer to validate that migrated data and systems are complete, compliant, and business-ready before cutover. The result is costly rollbacks, regulatory exposure, and eroded stakeholder confidence.

---

## 3. What Is Your Solution?

MAP Nexus™ provides a comprehensive assurance platform that validates, monitors, and certifies enterprise cloud migrations end-to-end. The platform delivers automated validation checks, real-time governance dashboards, and audit-ready compliance reporting — giving migration teams and executive stakeholders a single source of truth for migration confidence. Every migration validated. Every outcome assured.

---

## 4. What Is Your Market Opportunity?

The global cloud migration market is projected to exceed £150 billion by 2028, with financial services representing one of the highest-value, most compliance-intensive segments. UK financial institutions alone manage trillions in assets undergoing digital transformation, yet no dominant assurance platform exists to de-risk these programmes. MAP Nexus™ targets this gap with a focused, enterprise-grade solution.

---

## 5. Why Now?

Cloud migration has accelerated dramatically, yet migration failure rates remain stubbornly high. Regulatory bodies including the FCA and PRA are increasing scrutiny of operational resilience and third-party risk, creating urgent demand for auditable migration assurance. The convergence of regulatory pressure, legacy modernisation urgency, and cloud adoption creates a compelling window for MAP Nexus™.

---

## 6. Why Microsoft Azure?

Azure is the cloud platform of choice for the majority of UK financial services institutions, offering the compliance certifications, enterprise security, and hybrid capabilities this sector demands. MAP Nexus™ is built Azure-first to meet our customers where they operate, leveraging Azure's trusted infrastructure for performance, scalability, and regulatory alignment. Our architecture is designed to integrate seamlessly with Azure-native tooling and enterprise estates.

---

## 7. How Do You Use Azure Today?

MAP Nexus™ leverages a range of Azure services including Azure App Services, Azure SQL Database, Azure Functions, Azure Key Vault, Azure Monitor, and Azure DevOps for CI/CD. We utilise Azure Entra ID for identity and access management, Azure Blob Storage for data handling, and Azure API Management for secure service integration. Our platform is deployed across Azure regions to support enterprise-scale workloads.

---

## 8. What Is Your AI Strategy?

MAP Nexus™ incorporates intelligent automation to enhance validation accuracy, surface migration risks proactively, and deliver actionable insights to migration teams. Our AI strategy focuses on augmenting human decision-making — enabling faster risk identification, smarter remediation recommendations, and continuous learning from migration outcomes. We are committed to responsible AI practices aligned with Microsoft's responsible AI principles.
