# 08 — Founders Hub Application Responses

**MAP Nexus™ — Migration Assurance Platform**
**Microsoft Founders Hub Application v1.1**

---

## 1. Company Description

I'm Edward Odewale, founder of MAP (Migration Assurance Platform). We're building MAP Nexus™ — an Azure-first platform that validates, governs, and assures enterprise cloud migrations before they go live. We focus on financial services because that's where migration failure carries the highest cost: lost data, compliance exposure, and disrupted operations. Our job is to make sure none of that happens.

---

## 2. What Problem Are You Solving?

Up to 70% of enterprise cloud migrations fail or run into serious problems. Financial services firms are hit hardest — they're navigating strict FCA and PRA requirements while moving complex legacy systems that have been running for decades.

The core issue is simple: there's no unified, auditable layer that validates whether a migration is actually ready before it goes live. Organisations move fast, cross their fingers, and deal with the consequences — rollbacks, regulatory exposure, and eroded trust with stakeholders. MAP Nexus™ exists to fix that.

---

## 3. What Is Your Solution?

MAP Nexus™ is a pilot-ready assurance platform that validates, monitors, and certifies cloud migrations end-to-end. It sits between migration planning and production deployment — catching problems before they become costly failures.

Migration teams get automated validation checks, real-time governance dashboards, and audit-ready compliance reporting. Executives get a single source of truth. Every migration validated. Every outcome assured.

---

## 4. What Is Your Market Opportunity?

The global cloud migration market is projected to exceed £150 billion by 2028. Financial services is one of the highest-value, most compliance-intensive segments within that.

UK financial institutions manage trillions in assets that are actively undergoing digital transformation. Yet there's no dominant assurance platform that de-risks these programmes. We're targeting that gap with a focused, enterprise-grade solution purpose-built for regulated industries.

---

## 5. Why Now?

Cloud migration has accelerated dramatically, but failure rates haven't improved. Meanwhile, regulators like the FCA and PRA are tightening scrutiny on operational resilience and third-party risk — migration is increasingly on their radar.

That creates urgent demand for auditable migration assurance. The convergence of regulatory pressure, legacy modernisation urgency, and accelerating cloud adoption makes this the right time for MAP Nexus™.

---

## 6. Why Microsoft Azure?

Azure is the cloud platform of choice for the majority of UK financial services institutions. It offers the compliance certifications, enterprise security, and hybrid capabilities this sector demands.

We built MAP Nexus™ Azure-first because that's where our customers operate. Our platform integrates with Azure-native tooling and enterprise estates, leveraging Azure's trusted infrastructure for performance, scalability, and regulatory alignment.

---

## 7. How Do You Use Azure Today?

MAP Nexus™ runs on Azure Container Apps with Azure SQL Database for data persistence and Azure Cache for Redis for performance. We use Azure Entra ID for identity and access management, Azure Blob Storage for data handling, and Azure API Management for secure service integration. Azure Monitor handles observability, and Azure DevOps powers our CI/CD pipeline. Our platform is deployed across Azure regions to support enterprise-scale workloads.

---

## 8. What Is Your AI Strategy?

MAP Nexus™ incorporates intelligent automation to enhance validation accuracy, surface migration risks proactively, and deliver actionable insights. Our focus is on augmenting human decision-making — faster risk identification, smarter remediation recommendations, and continuous learning from migration outcomes.

We're committed to responsible AI practices aligned with Microsoft's responsible AI principles.
