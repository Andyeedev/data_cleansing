## 1. Purpose

This document defines the comprehensive Customer Success Framework for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) Pilot Deployment. It establishes the methodology, processes, and standards that govern how customer success is delivered to pilot customers, ensuring they achieve their desired business outcomes while maximizing the value of their MAP investment.

The framework is designed to:
- Proactively guide customers toward successful outcomes
- Measure and optimize customer health throughout the lifecycle
- Create a structured approach to customer engagement
- Identify and mitigate risks before they impact customer relationships
- Drive expansion and advocacy from successful pilot deployments
- Build a scalable foundation for post-pilot customer success operations

---

## 2. Scope

This framework applies to:
- All MAP pilot customers and their stakeholders
- Customer Success Managers (CSMs) assigned to pilot accounts
- Sales Engineering and Support teams supporting pilot customers
- Executive sponsors and stakeholders
- All customer touchpoints throughout the pilot lifecycle

## 3. Success Methodology

### 3.1 Customer Success vs Customer Support

| Aspect | Customer Success | Customer Support |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| **Focus** | Proactive, outcome-focused | Reactive, issue-focused |
| **Goal** | Drive business value and outcomes | Resolve technical issues |
| **Timing** | Ongoing throughout lifecycle | As-needed basis |
| **Approach** | Strategic partnership | Tactical assistance |
| **Metrics** | Business outcomes, adoption, retention | Resolution time, satisfaction |
| **Engagement** | Scheduled, proactive | As-requested, reactive |

### 3.2 Customer Success Principles

**Principle 1: Outcome-Driven**
- Focus on customer business outcomes, not just product usage
- Align MAP capabilities with customer success criteria
- Measure and communicate value delivered

**Principle 2: Proactive Engagement**
- Anticipate customer needs before they arise
- Identify risks and opportunities early
- Provide guidance before problems occur

**Principle 3: Data-Informed**
- Use data to drive decisions and recommendations
- Track adoption and usage patterns
- Measure customer health objectively

**Principle 4: Personalized Approach**
- Tailor engagement to customer needs and goals
- Adapt strategy based on customer maturity
- Recognize unique customer contexts

**Principle 5: Continuous Improvement**
- Learn from each customer interaction
- Refine processes based on outcomes
- Share best practices across the customer base

## 4. Customer Health Score

### 4.1 Health Score Components

**Overall Health Score = (Usage Score × 0.3) + (Satisfaction Score × 0.25) + (Support Score × 0.2) + (Adoption Score × 0.25)**

| Component | Weight | Measurement | Data Source |
|-----------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Usage Score** | 30% | Active usage metrics | Product analytics |
| **Satisfaction Score** | 25% | Customer feedback | Surveys, interviews |
| **Support Score** | 20% | Support interaction quality | Ticketing system |
| **Adoption Score** | 25% | Feature adoption depth | Product analytics |

### 4.2 Usage Score Calculation

**Usage Metrics:**

| Metric | Weight | Scoring Criteria |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Daily Active Users** | 40% | >80% = 100, 60-80% = 75, 40-60% = 50, <40% = 25 |
| **Session Duration** | 30% | >30 min = 100, 20-30 min = 75, 10-20 min = 50, <10 min = 25 |
| **Feature Utilization** | 30% | >70% features = 100, 50-70% = 75, 30-50% = 50, <30% = 25 |

**Usage Score Ranges:**
- **Healthy (80-100):** Strong, consistent usage patterns
- **Moderate (60-79):** Acceptable usage with room for improvement
- **At Risk (40-59):** Declining or inconsistent usage
- **Critical (<40):** Severely low or declining usage

### 4.3 Satisfaction Score Calculation

**Satisfaction Metrics:**

| Metric | Weight | Data Collection | Scoring |
|--------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **NPS Score** | 40% | Quarterly survey | Promoter=100, Passive=50, Detractor=0 |
| **CSAT Score** | 35% | Post-interaction survey | 5=100, 4=75, 3=50, 2=25, 1=0 |
| **CES Score** | 25% | Post-interaction survey | 7=100, 6=85, 5=70, 4=55, 3=40, 2=25, 1=10 |

**Satisfaction Score Ranges:**
- **Healthy (80-100):** Highly satisfied, likely advocates
- **Moderate (60-79):** Generally satisfied with concerns
- **At Risk (40-59):** Dissatisfied, risk of churn
- **Critical (<40):** Highly dissatisfied, immediate intervention required

### 4.4 Support Score Calculation

**Support Metrics:**

| Metric | Weight | Scoring Criteria |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Ticket Volume** | 30% | <5/month=100, 5-10/month=75, 10-20/month=50, >20/month=25 |
| **Resolution Time** | 30% | <SLA=100, Within SLA=75, 1.5x SLA=50, >1.5x SLA=25 |
| **Escalation Rate** | 20% | <5%=100, 5-10%=75, 10-20%=50, >20%=25 |
| **Customer Effort** | 20% | CES score (see satisfaction metrics) |

**Support Score Ranges:**
- **Healthy (80-100):** Minimal issues, quick resolution
- **Moderate (60-79):** Some issues, acceptable resolution
- **At Risk (40-59):** Frequent issues, slow resolution
- **Critical (<40):** Constant issues, poor resolution

### 4.5 Adoption Score Calculation

**Adoption Metrics:**

| Metric | Weight | Scoring Criteria |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Feature Depth** | 40% | >70% features used=100, 50-70%=75, 30-50%=50, <30%=25 |
| **Workflow Completion** | 30% | >80% workflows=100, 60-80%=75, 40-60%=50, <40%=25 |
| **Integration Usage** | 30% | All integrations=100, Most=75, Some=50, Few=25 |

**Adoption Score Ranges:**
- **Healthy (80-100):** Deep adoption across features and workflows
- **Moderate (60-79):** Good adoption with expansion opportunities
- **At Risk (40-59):** Shallow adoption, limited value realization
- **Critical (<40):** Minimal adoption, significant value gap

### 4.6 Health Score Interpretation

| Health Score | Status | Action Required | CSM Response |
|--------------|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **80-100** | Healthy | Maintain and expand | Regular check-ins, expansion opportunities |
| **60-79** | Moderate | Optimize and grow | Proactive guidance, adoption acceleration |
| **40-59** | At Risk | Intervene and save | Escalation, success plan, executive alignment |
| **<40** | Critical | Rescue or transition | Immediate intervention, executive escalation |
