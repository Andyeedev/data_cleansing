# Founder - Medium Biography

The founder of MAP Nexus™ is a technology executive specialising in enterprise data migration, governance, and validation. With extensive experience in regulated industries, they have witnessed firsthand the consequences of unvalidated data migrations: regulatory penalties, customer harm, and wasted investment.

MAP Nexus™ was founded to eliminate migration risk. The platform provides automated validation, executive dashboards, and governance frameworks that give organisations absolute confidence in their migrated data.

As a Microsoft Founders Hub participant, MAP Nexus™ is built on the Azure platform, leveraging enterprise-grade security, compliance, and scale. The founder's vision is to make MAP Nexus™ the global standard for migration assurance - the platform every enterprise trusts to validate, govern, and protect their most critical data assets.

The founder is available for speaking engagements, industry panels, and enterprise consultations on migration governance and data integrity.
