# 01 — Executive Summary

**MAP Nexus™ — Migration Assurance Platform**
**Microsoft Founders Hub Application v1.1**

---

## Company

**Migration Assurance Platform Ltd** (proposed)
Brand: **MAP**
Product: **MAP Nexus™**
Domain: mapnexus.com

---

## Mission

To eliminate migration failures through automated validation, governance, and continuous assurance — giving enterprises confidence before every go-live.

---

## Vision

To become the global standard for migration assurance — the trusted checkpoint that every enterprise migration must pass through.

---

## The Problem

Enterprise cloud migrations fail at alarming rates. Organisations across financial services, government, insurance, healthcare, and other regulated industries face:

- **Data loss** — incomplete or inaccurate migrations compromise critical business data.
- **Unplanned downtime** — failed deployments disrupt operations and erode stakeholder confidence.
- **Compliance exposure** — audit gaps and governance failures invite regulatory scrutiny.
- **Cost overruns** — remediation of migration defects consumes budget and delays programmes.

Current tooling focuses on moving data. Almost nothing focuses on validating it before it goes live.

---

## The Solution

MAP Nexus™ is a pilot-ready enterprise assurance platform that sits between migration planning and production deployment. It validates, governs, and assures migrations before go-live — reducing risk, accelerating delivery, and giving executives the visibility they need.

---

## Market Positioning

MAP sits at the intersection of **cloud migration tooling** and **enterprise governance, risk, and compliance (GRC)**. Where existing tools focus on moving workloads, MAP provides the validation and assurance layer that ensures those workloads are ready for production. This positions MAP as a complementary platform to migration tooling — not a competitor — and as a differentiated entrant in the migration governance space.

---

## Market Opportunity

The enterprise cloud migration market is projected to exceed **$100 billion by 2027**. UK financial services institutions alone manage thousands of active migration programmes, each requiring validation, governance, and regulatory assurance. MAP targets this market with a differentiated, Azure-first platform purpose-built for regulated industries.

---

## Business Model

MAP operates on a **SaaS subscription model** supported by professional services including implementation, advisory, and managed validation engagements.

---

## Current Stage

MAP has a **pilot-ready MVP** developed over seven sprints with a **seven-person team**. The platform is Azure-native, built on Microsoft Entra ID, Azure SQL, Azure Storage, and Azure Monitor. MAP is currently engaging early adopters in financial services and is seeking partnership through the Microsoft Founders Hub to accelerate commercialisation and enterprise adoption.

---

## Future Direction

MAP's roadmap includes AI-powered validation capabilities, multi-cloud support, and enterprise marketplace distribution — positioning the platform as the definitive standard for migration assurance across global regulated markets.
