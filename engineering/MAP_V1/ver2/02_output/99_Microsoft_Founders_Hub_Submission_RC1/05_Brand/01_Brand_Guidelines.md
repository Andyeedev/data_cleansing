## Logo Usage

- Primary logo: MAP Nexus™ wordmark with icon
- Minimum size: 120px wide (digital), 30mm (print)
- Clear space: minimum 1x logo height on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, or alter logo proportions
- Use approved colour variants only

---

## Voice & Tone

- Executive-level language
- Confident, not arrogant
- Technical accuracy with business clarity
- Avoid jargon unless addressing technical audiences
- Use "Validation Exception" not "Failed"
- Use "Attention Required" not "FAIL"
