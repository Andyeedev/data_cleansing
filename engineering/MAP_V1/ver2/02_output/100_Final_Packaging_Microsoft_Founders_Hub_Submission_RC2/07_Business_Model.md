# Business Model — Migration Assurance Platform

## Customer Segments

MAP serves three primary market segments:

| Segment | Profile |
|---|---|
| **Enterprise Financial Services** | Tier 1–3 banks, insurers, and asset managers with complex, regulated migration requirements |
| **Mid-Market Organisations** | Firms with 500–5,000 employees undergoing digital transformation or platform consolidation |
| **Professional Services Firms** | Consultancies and system integrators requiring repeatable migration validation tooling for client engagements |

Our initial focus is the UK financial services sector, where regulatory pressure and legacy estate complexity create urgent demand for assured migration outcomes.

## SaaS Subscription Model

MAP is delivered as a multi-tenant SaaS platform with tiered subscription pricing:

| Tier | Target Customer | Key Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Mid-market, early-stage migrations | Core validation workflows, standard reporting, email support |
| **Professional** | Enterprise, complex programmes | Advanced analytics, custom workflows, dedicated support, API access |
| **Enterprise** | Regulated, multi-programme | Full platform capabilities, SLA guarantees, priority support, compliance reporting |

Subscriptions are annual, with usage-based components for high-volume processing tiers.

## Professional Services

Beyond SaaS subscriptions, MAP offers professional services to accelerate customer value:

- **Implementation Services**: Platform configuration, integration setup, and migration validation framework design
- **Training & Enablement**: Structured programmes for internal teams to maximise platform adoption
- **Consulting & Advisory**: Expert guidance on migration strategy, risk assessment, and regulatory compliance

Professional services engagements are scoped individually and designed to complement SaaS adoption.

## Future AI Offerings

As MAP matures, we will introduce AI-powered capabilities built on Azure OpenAI:

- **Intelligent Validation Insights**: Automated analysis of migration patterns to surface risks and recommendations
- **Predictive Risk Scoring**: Data-driven assessment of migration readiness and potential outcomes
- **Natural Language Reporting**: Executive-friendly narrative summaries of validation results

These capabilities will be delivered as premium add-ons within the subscription model, creating expansion revenue opportunities.

## Revenue Model Summary

MAP's business model is designed for scalable, recurring revenue:

- **Recurring Revenue**: Tiered SaaS subscriptions provide predictable baseline revenue
- **Expansion Revenue**: Professional services and AI add-ons drive net revenue retention above 120%
- **Land and Expand**: Start with single-team adoption, expand across programmes and geographies

Our model is built to scale with customer success — as customers migrate more, they consume more, and MAP delivers increasing value.
