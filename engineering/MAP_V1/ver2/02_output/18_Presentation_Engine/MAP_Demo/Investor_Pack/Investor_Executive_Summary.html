The Problem

Enterprise migrations fail silently. Without governance and validation, organisations migrate data that is incomplete, inconsistent, or non-compliant. The result: regulatory penalties, customer harm, and wasted investment. Migration tools move data. Nothing validates that it arrived correctly.

Migration Risk

Data loss, corruption, and schema drift silently destroy business value during migration.

Governance Gaps

No visibility into what migrated, what failed, or what needs remediation.

Compliance Exposure

Regulatory frameworks demand evidence of data integrity. Gaps create audit failures.

Executive Uncertainty

Leadership cannot manage what they cannot see. Without dashboards, risk is invisible.
