Company

MAP Nexus™ is developed by a team of enterprise data professionals committed to transforming how organizations approach data migration assurance. Our platform addresses the critical gap between data migration execution and validation governance.

Mission

To empower every organization with reliable, auditable, and intelligent data migration assurance that eliminates risk and ensures business continuity.

Vision

To become the industry standard for data migration validation, making zero-risk migration a reality for enterprises worldwide.

Business Problem

Enterprise data migrations fail at an alarming rate. Studies indicate that 60-80% of data migration projects experience significant issues, including data loss, corruption, and extended downtime. The root cause is not migration technology — it is the absence of systematic validation and governance during the migration process.

Organizations lack real-time visibility into data quality during migration, cannot audit validation results, and have no automated way to enforce governance policies. This leads to costly post-migration remediation, regulatory non-compliance, and business disruption.

MAP Solution

MAP Nexus™ (Migration Assurance Platform) provides end-to-end validation and governance for data migrations. Our platform automates the entire validation lifecycle — from rule discovery and control execution to governance decision-making and executive reporting.

  • Automated Validation: Execute validation controls automatically against source and target data
  • Governance Integration: Real-time governance decisions with full audit trail
  • Executive Visibility: Dashboard and reporting for stakeholders at every level
  • Risk Assessment: Automated risk scoring with go/no-go recommendations
  • Data Quality Analysis: Multi-dimensional quality assessment with trend tracking

Benefits

Reduce Risk

Eliminate data loss and corruption through automated validation controls executed before, during, and after migration.

Ensure Compliance

Maintain full audit trail of all validation activities with governance decision tracking for regulatory requirements.

Accelerate Migration

Reduce migration timelines by 40-60% through automated validation and real-time issue detection.

Improve Quality

Multi-dimensional data quality analysis ensures data meets business requirements post-migration.
