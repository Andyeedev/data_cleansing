# Founder - Press Biography

The founder of MAP Nexus is a technology executive specialising in enterprise data migration, governance, and validation. As a Microsoft Founders Hub participant, they are building the global standard for migration assurance on the Azure platform.

MAP Nexus provides automated validation, executive dashboards, and governance frameworks for organisations navigating complex data migrations. The platform serves regulated industries including financial services, government, and healthcare.
