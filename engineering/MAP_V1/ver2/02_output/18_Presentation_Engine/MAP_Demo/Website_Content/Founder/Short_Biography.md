# Founder - Short Biography

The founder of MAP Nexus is a technology executive with deep expertise in enterprise data migration, governance, and validation. As a Microsoft Founders Hub participant, they are building the global standard for migration assurance on the Azure platform.

MAP Nexus was born from first-hand experience: seeing organisations invest millions in migrations only to discover, too late, that critical data was lost, corrupted, or non-compliant.

The founder's vision is simple: every enterprise migration should be validated, governed, and assured. MAP Nexus makes that possible.
