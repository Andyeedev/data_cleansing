# Why MAP Nexus

Every enterprise migration carries risk. Data loss, corruption, and governance gaps silently destroy business value. Organisations discover the damage too late - when executives lose confidence, regulators demand answers, or customers are harmed.

Migration tools move data. Nothing validates that it arrived correctly.
