# Governance Centre

## Overview
Built-in governance controls, approval workflows, and audit trails ensure every migration action is documented and compliant.

## Business Problem
Governance is often bolted on after migration. Manual documentation is inconsistent, incomplete, and fails audit scrutiny.

## Solution
MAP embeds governance in every migration action:
- Approval workflows
- Audit trails
- Compliance documentation
- Decision support
- Risk classification

## Benefits
- Governance built-in, not bolted on
- Audit-ready from day one
- Automated documentation
- Decision support for programme directors
- Regulatory compliance

## Customer Outcomes
- Every action documented
- Every decision auditable
- Every compliance requirement satisfied
