# Risk Assessment

Automated risk identification with severity classification. Every exception is categorised, explained, and linked to remediation guidance.

## Solution

- Critical issue identification
- High/medium/low classification
- Business impact assessment
- Remediation guidance
- Risk trending

- Business impact focus
- Actionable remediation
- Risk visibility for leadership
- Compliance evidence

- Clear remediation path
- Business decisions informed by risk
