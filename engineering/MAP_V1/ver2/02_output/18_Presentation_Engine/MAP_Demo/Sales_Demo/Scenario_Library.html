7 Industry Scenarios

Each scenario reuses the same dashboards with different narration and business context. Adapt the demo to your audience's industry.

1. Financial Services

Business Context

Banks and financial institutions migrate core banking systems, customer data, and transaction records. Regulatory requirements demand absolute data integrity.

Key Pain Points

  • Regulatory compliance (FCA, PRA, Basel III)
  • Customer data integrity during core banking migration
  • Transaction record validation
  • Audit trail requirements

Suggested Narration

"For financial services, data integrity is non-negotiable. MAP Nexus validates every record, every balance, and every relationship during migration. Regulatory evidence is generated automatically."
