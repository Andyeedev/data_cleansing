2. Demonstration Flow (30-Minute Standard)

2 minutes - Opening

Welcome and Problem Statement

Introduce MAP Nexus, explain the migration challenge, and establish customer pain. Focus on business risk, not technical issues.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Establish relevance and urgency.

Narration: "Every enterprise migration carries risk. Data loss, governance gaps, and compliance failures can silently destroy business value. MAP Nexus exists to eliminate that risk."

Key Messages:

  • Migration failure is invisible until it causes business harm
  • Existing tools move data but do not validate outcomes
  • Executives need visibility, not just reports

Questions to Ask: "Have you experienced data issues after a migration? How did you discover them?"

2 minutes - Product Introduction

MAP Nexus Overview

Introduce MAP Nexus purpose, business value, and where it fits in the migration lifecycle. Never explain implementation details.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Position MAP as the solution.

Narration: "MAP Nexus is an enterprise migration governance and validation platform. It validates that your migrated data is accurate, complete, and compliant."

Key Messages:

  • Complementary to migration tools, not competitive
  • Zero silent data loss, 100% validation coverage
  • Built on Azure for enterprise security
2 minutes - Landing Page

Platform Entry Point

Open the Landing Page. Explain migration scenario, execution status, readiness score, and navigation.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Show the platform entry point.

Narration: "This is your migration command centre. At a glance, you can see your execution status, readiness score, and navigation to every module."

Key Messages:

  • Central navigation hub for all migration governance
  • Key performance indicators visible immediately
  • Easy access to all platform modules
5 minutes - Executive Dashboard

Executive Visibility

Show overall migration readiness, programme health, executive KPIs, and business decision support.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Demonstrate executive value.

Narration: "This dashboard gives your C-suite real-time visibility into migration health. Validation status, risk posture, and business outcomes are visible at a glance."

Key Messages:

  • No surprises for leadership
  • Data-driven decision support
  • Business outcomes, not technical metrics

Questions to Ask: "How do your executives currently track migration progress?"

3 minutes - Migration Overview

Migration Progress

Show batch-level migration status, programme visibility, and delivery confidence.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Show operational visibility.

Narration: "Here you can see exactly what has migrated, what is in progress, and what needs attention. Batch-level tracking with exception highlighting."

Key Messages:

  • Complete visibility into migration progress
  • Exception highlighting for rapid remediation
  • Delivery confidence for programme directors
5 minutes - Validation Centre

Validation Execution

Show validation execution, business assurance, readiness scoring, and executive confidence.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Demonstrate core validation capability.

Narration: "This is the heart of MAP. Every validation control is executed, tracked, and reported. Executive-friendly status labels - PASS, FAIL, ERROR, BLOCKED - no technical jargon."

Key Messages:

  • Automated validation eliminates manual checks
  • Executive-friendly language for all outcomes
  • Every validation is auditable and traceable
4 minutes - Governance Centre

Governance and Compliance

Show approval workflows, audit trails, compliance status, and decision support.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Demonstrate governance capability.

Narration: "MAP provides built-in governance. Approval workflows, audit trails, and compliance evidence are generated automatically. No manual documentation required."

Key Messages:

  • Governance is built-in, not bolted on
  • Audit-ready evidence for regulators
  • Decision support for programme directors
3 minutes - Data Quality

Data Quality Profiling

Show completeness, accuracy, consistency, and business confidence metrics.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Show data quality visibility.

Narration: "MAP profiles your data quality across four dimensions: completeness, accuracy, consistency, and timeliness. You can see exactly where data quality issues exist."

Key Messages:

  • Proactive data quality identification
  • Business-friendly quality metrics
  • Remediation guidance for every issue
4 minutes - Risk Assessment

Risk Identification

Show critical issues, high risk, medium risk, business impact, and mitigation guidance.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Demonstrate risk management.

Narration: "Every exception is categorised by severity and business impact. Critical issues are highlighted immediately. Mitigation guidance is provided for every risk."

Key Messages:

  • Automated risk classification
  • Business impact assessment
  • Actionable mitigation guidance
3 minutes - Reports

Executive Reporting

Show executive reporting, governance evidence, audit support, and PDF export capability.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Show reporting capability.

Narration: "MAP generates eight executive-ready HTML reports. Every report is audit-ready, governance-ready, and suitable for board meetings or regulatory submissions."

Key Messages:

  • Eight comprehensive reports generated automatically
  • Audit-ready for regulators
  • PDF export for distribution
2 minutes - Closing

Summary and Next Steps

Summarise business outcomes, customer benefits, and agree next steps.

Presenter Notes

Purpose: Close with value and call to action.

Narration: "MAP Nexus eliminates migration risk, gives executives confidence, and provides audit-ready governance. The next step is a proof of value with your data."

Key Messages:

  • Zero silent data loss
  • Executive confidence from day one
  • Ready for proof of value

3. Demonstration Checklist

Before Demo

  • Confirm audience type and duration
  • Open demo launcher in browser
  • Verify offline mode is available
  • Set browser to full screen (F11)
  • Resolution: 1920x1080 or 1280x720
  • Backup PDF screenshots available
  • FAQ and objection responses prepared
  • Customer-specific talking points ready

During Demo

  • Start with problem statement, not features
  • Ask questions to engage audience
  • Adapt narration to audience type
  • Highlight business outcomes, not technical details
  • Watch for signs of disengagement
  • Be prepared to skip sections for time

After Demo

  • Summarise key business outcomes
  • Agree specific next steps
  • Send follow-up materials within 24 hours
  • Record demo outcomes in CRM
  • Debrief with team on lessons learned
