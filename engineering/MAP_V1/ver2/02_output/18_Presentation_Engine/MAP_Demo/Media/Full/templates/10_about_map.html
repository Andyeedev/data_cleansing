Company

MAP Nexus™ is developed by a team of enterprise data professionals committed to transforming how organizations approach data migration assurance.

Mission

To empower every organization with reliable, auditable, and intelligent data migration assurance that eliminates risk and ensures business continuity.

Vision

To become the industry standard for data migration validation, making zero-risk migration a reality for enterprises worldwide.

Business Problem

Enterprise data migrations fail at an alarming rate. 60-80% of data migration projects experience significant issues including data loss, corruption, and extended downtime. The root cause is the absence of systematic validation and governance during migration.

MAP Solution

MAP Nexus™ provides end-to-end validation and governance for data migrations. Our platform automates the entire validation lifecycle — from rule discovery and control execution to governance decision-making and executive reporting.
