# Executive Summary

## Industry Focus

Banking and Financial Services

Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) is purpose-built to address one of the most critical challenges facing financial institutions:

> Modernising legacy systems without introducing operational, regulatory, or customer risk.

Banks continue to invest billions in transformation programmes, including:

* Core banking replacement
* Payments modernisation
* Cloud migration
* Data platform transformation
* Regulatory reporting upgrades
* Digital banking initiatives

Despite these investments, migration risk remains one of the largest causes of programme delays, cost overruns, regulatory scrutiny, and customer impact.

MAP provides the assurance and intelligence layer that helps financial institutions modernise with confidence.
