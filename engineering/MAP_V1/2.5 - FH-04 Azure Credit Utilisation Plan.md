# Executive Summary

This document outlines how Microsoft Founders Hub Azure Credits will be utilised to accelerate development, testing, validation, and commercialisation of the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The objective is not simply to consume Azure services, but to establish a scalable Azure-native platform capable of supporting enterprise migration modernisation programmes.

Azure credits will primarily support:

* Product development
* Platform validation
* AI experimentation
* Pilot customer environments
* SaaS readiness
* Marketplace preparation

---

# Azure Utilisation Strategy

MAP will adopt Azure incrementally through three stages.

---

## Stage 1 – Product Development

Purpose:

Build and validate the platform.

## Stage 3 – Commercial Readiness
