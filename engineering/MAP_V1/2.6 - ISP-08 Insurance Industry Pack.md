# Executive Summary

## Industry Focus

Insurance & Reinsurance

Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) helps insurance organisations modernise complex legacy systems while maintaining regulatory compliance, operational resilience, and customer trust.

Insurance organisations face increasing pressure to:

* Modernise policy administration platforms
* Improve claims processing capabilities
* Adopt cloud technologies
* Enhance customer experiences
* Improve data governance
* Enable AI and advanced analytics

Despite significant investment in transformation programmes, migration remains one of the most challenging and risky activities within the insurance sector.

MAP provides the assurance and intelligence layer that enables insurers to modernise with confidence.
