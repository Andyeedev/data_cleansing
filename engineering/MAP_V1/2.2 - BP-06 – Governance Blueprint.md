# 1. Purpose

The Governance Blueprint defines **how products are governed throughout their lifecycle**.

It ensures every product:

* aligns to enterprise strategy
* complies with architecture standards
* follows security requirements
* manages risk
* remains commercially viable
* has clear ownership
* undergoes structured decision making

This blueprint is the operating model for product governance.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Governance Blueprint ensures:

* consistent decision making
* clear accountability
* controlled change
* regulatory compliance
* investment oversight
* portfolio visibility
* architectural consistency

# 4. Governance Principles

Every decision should satisfy:

### Strategic Alignment

Supports enterprise objectives.

---

### Customer Value

Improves customer outcomes.

---

### Financial Value

Produces measurable business value.

---

### Risk Awareness

Risks are identified and mitigated.

---

### Compliance

Meets internal and external obligations.

---

### Simplicity

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

### Transparency

All decisions are documented.

---

### Accountability

Every decision has an accountable owner.

# 5. Governance Decision Matrix

| Decision           | Product | Architecture | Security | Executive |
| ------------------ | ------- | ------------ | -------- | --------- |
| New Product        | A       | C            | C        | A         |
| Major Investment   | C       | C            | C        | A         |
| Feature Release    | A       | C            | C        | I         |
| Technical Standard | I       | A            | C        | I         |
| Security Exception | I       | C            | A        | I         |
| Product Retirement | A       | C            | C        | A         |
| Budget Approval    | C       | I            | I        | A         |
| Vendor Selection   | A       | C            | C        | C         |

Legend:

* **A** = Accountable
* **C** = Consulted
* **I** = Informed
