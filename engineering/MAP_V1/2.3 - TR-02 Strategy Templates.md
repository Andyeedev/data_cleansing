**Category:** Strategy
**Owner:** Product Strategy Office / Product Operations

---

# 1. Purpose

The Strategy Template Library provides a standardized set of reusable templates that support product strategy development, strategic planning, business justification, and executive decision-making.

These templates ensure that all products within the Product Operating System (POS) are planned, evaluated, and governed using a consistent strategic framework.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Strategy Template Library aims to:

* Standardize strategic planning documentation.
* Improve the quality and consistency of strategy artefacts.
* Accelerate product planning and approval.
* Support governance and investment decisions.
* Align product initiatives with enterprise strategy.
* Facilitate collaboration across business and technology teams.
* Reduce duplication of effort.
* Maintain traceability from strategy through execution.

---

# 3. Scope

The Strategy Template Library covers templates used throughout the strategic product lifecycle, including:

* Product vision
* Product strategy
* Product charter
* Business case
* Product portfolio proposals
* Investment requests
* Opportunity assessments
* Market analysis
* Customer value propositions
* Strategic roadmaps
* Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)
* Product lifecycle planning

# 5. Standard Template Structure

Every strategy template shall contain the following sections where applicable:

## Document Information

* Template ID
* Template Name
* Version
* Owner
* Approval Status
* Effective Date
* Review Date

---

## Purpose

Why the template exists.

---

## When to Use

The point within the product lifecycle where the template should be applied.

---

## Intended Audience

Typical users include:

* Product Managers
* Product Owners
* Portfolio Managers
* Strategy Office
* Executive Sponsors
* Business Analysts
* Enterprise Architects
* Finance Partners

---

## Required Inputs

List of prerequisite information or supporting artefacts.

---

## Instructions

Guidance on how to complete the template, including mandatory fields and examples where appropriate.

---

## Template Body

The structured sections, tables, prompts, and fields to be completed.

---

## Review & Approval

Details of required reviewers, approvers, and governance checkpoints.

---

## Related Documents

References to associated Product Canon, Blueprint, and Template documents.

---

# 6. Template Design Principles

All strategy templates shall be:

* Standardized
* Modular
* Easy to complete
* Governance-ready
* Outcome-focused
* Reusable
* Version controlled
* Digitally compatible

---

# 7. Governance

The Product Strategy Office is responsible for:

* Maintaining strategy templates.
* Reviewing and approving updates.
* Ensuring alignment with the Product Canon.
* Retiring obsolete templates.
* Publishing approved versions.

# 10. Success Measures

The Strategy Template Library is successful when:

* All strategic planning uses approved templates.
* Product proposals are consistent and complete.
* Governance reviews require fewer revisions.
* Strategic decisions are supported by standardized evidence.
* Strategy artefacts are reusable across the enterprise.
* Traceability exists from product vision through execution and measurement.
