# DP-01 – Delivery Strategy & Approach

## Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)

Version 1.0
Status: Complete

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Delivery Strategy & Approach for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP) Release 1.

It establishes the delivery methodology, execution model, governance approach, planning principles, and delivery controls that will guide the successful build and deployment of the MAP MVP.

The strategy ensures alignment between:

* Business objectives
* Product vision
* Technical architecture
* Security requirements
* Governance controls
* Delivery timelines
