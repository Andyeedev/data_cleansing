**Migration Assurance Platform (MAP)**
**Phase 2.4 – Commercial & Growth Framework**

**Document ID:** CG-09
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Complete

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic partnership model for the Migration Assurance Platform (MAP).

The objective is to establish a structured framework that enables MAP to scale through partnerships rather than relying solely on direct sales and internal delivery resources.

The framework supports:

* Market access
* Customer acquisition
* Product credibility
* Technical enablement
* Cloud ecosystem alignment
* Industry influence

---

# 2. Strategic Principle

MAP is not intended to compete with:

* System integrators
* Consulting firms
* Cloud providers
* Migration delivery partners

Instead MAP complements them.

---

### Positioning Statement

> Migration partners deliver transformation. MAP provides assurance.

This distinction must remain consistent across all commercial activities.

---

# 3. Partnership Objectives

The partnership strategy has five primary objectives.

---

## Objective 1 – Accelerate Customer Acquisition

Partners already have trusted relationships with enterprise customers.

MAP leverages these relationships to reduce acquisition costs.

---

## Objective 2 – Increase Market Credibility

Partnerships with recognised organisations strengthen customer confidence.

Examples:

* Microsoft
* Financial services consultancies
* Migration specialists

---

## Objective 3 – Extend Delivery Capacity

Partners provide implementation and migration expertise.

This allows MAP to remain focused on product development.

---

## Objective 4 – Enable Ecosystem Growth

Partners create repeatable sales channels.

---

## Objective 5 – Support International Expansion

Regional partners enable market entry without significant internal expansion.

---

# 8. Joint Solution Model

The preferred delivery model is collaborative.

---

### Customer

Requires migration.

↓

### Partner

Executes migration programme.

↓

### MAP

Provides assurance and governance.

↓

### Cloud Provider

Provides infrastructure.

---

This creates clear separation of responsibilities.
