# 1. Purpose

The Planning Template Library provides a standardized collection of templates used to transform approved product strategies and validated discovery outputs into executable plans.

The templates support planning across product, programme, portfolio, and migration initiatives, ensuring consistency, governance, and traceability from strategy through delivery.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Planning Template Library aims to:

* Standardize planning activities.
* Translate strategy into executable work.
* Improve planning quality and consistency.
* Support governance and investment decisions.
* Align resources with business priorities.
* Enable effective scheduling and dependency management.
* Improve forecasting and delivery confidence.
* Maintain end-to-end traceability.

---

# 3. Scope

Planning templates support:

* Product planning
* Migration planning
* Programme planning
* Portfolio planning
* Release planning
* Resource planning
* Dependency management
* Milestone planning
* Delivery forecasting
* Risk-based planning

# 5. Standard Template Structure

Each planning template should include:

## Document Information

* Template ID
* Template Name
* Version
* Owner
* Approval Status
* Effective Date
* Review Date

---

## Purpose

Defines the planning objective supported by the template.

---

## When to Use

Indicates where the template fits within the planning lifecycle.

---

## Intended Audience

Typical users include:

* Product Managers
* Programme Managers
* Project Managers
* Product Owners
* Delivery Managers
* Solution Architects
* Enterprise Architects
* PMO
* Executive Sponsors

---

## Required Inputs

Typical inputs include:

* Approved Product Strategy
* Discovery Summary
* Business Case
* Architecture Blueprint
* Resource Availability
* Budget Approval
* Risk Register
* Governance Decisions

---

## Instructions

Guidance for completing the template, including mandatory sections and examples where appropriate.

---

## Template Body

Structured fields, schedules, tables, diagrams, or planning artefacts.

---

## Review & Approval

Defines required governance reviews, approvals, and planning gates.

---

## Related Documents

Links to Product Canon, Blueprint Library, Discovery Templates, Delivery Templates, and Governance Templates.

# 6. Planning Lifecycle

Planning follows a structured lifecycle:

1. Review approved strategy.
2. Analyse discovery outputs.
3. Define scope.
4. Estimate effort and resources.
5. Develop roadmap.
6. Identify dependencies.
7. Assess risks.
8. Establish milestones.
9. Validate budget and capacity.
10. Obtain governance approval.
11. Baseline the plan.
12. Monitor and update throughout delivery.

---

# 7. Design Principles

Planning templates should be:

* Outcome-focused
* Realistic
* Evidence-based
* Adaptable
* Traceable
* Collaborative
* Risk-aware
* Governance-ready
* Scalable
* Vendor-neutral

---

# 8. Governance

The Product Management Office is responsible for:

* Maintaining planning templates.
* Reviewing updates.
* Ensuring alignment with enterprise planning standards.
* Publishing approved versions.
* Retiring superseded templates.

# 11. Success Measures

The Planning Template Library is successful when:

* Plans are complete and consistently structured.
* Delivery teams understand scope, milestones, and responsibilities.
* Dependencies and risks are visible.
* Resource allocation aligns with priorities.
* Governance approvals are supported by high-quality planning artefacts.
* Forecast accuracy improves over time.
* Business outcomes remain traceable from strategy through execution.
