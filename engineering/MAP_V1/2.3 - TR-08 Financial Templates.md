# 1. Purpose

The Financial Templates provide a standard set of commercial, budgeting, forecasting, costing, business case, and investment artefacts used during Migration Assurance Platform engagements.

These templates support:

* Internal investment decisions
* Client business cases
* Migration programme budgeting
* Vendor funding applications
* Investor readiness
* Founders Hub applications
* ROI modelling
* Commercial governance
