# 1. MAP Nexus Positioning

## Company

**MAP Nexus**

## Tagline / descriptor

**Migration Assurance & Data Validation**

## Core proposition

MAP Nexus is the validation checkpoint for data migrations.

The public proposition is that MAP Nexus helps organisations establish,
with evidence, that migrated data is:

-   Correct
-   Complete
-   Reconciled
-   Structurally valid
-   Ready for cutover

MAP Nexus is in **early market validation**.

Financial services is an important early validation segment, but MAP
Nexus is **not restricted to financial services**. Other relevant
enterprise sectors discussed include:

-   Pharmaceuticals
-   Government
-   Housing
-   Insurance
-   Healthcare
-   Education
-   Other enterprise organisations undertaking significant data
    migrations
