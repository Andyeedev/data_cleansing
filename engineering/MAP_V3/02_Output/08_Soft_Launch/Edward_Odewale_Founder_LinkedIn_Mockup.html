About

Founder @ MAP Nexus — Migration Assurance & Data Validation We help organisations answer a simple question before go-live: is the migrated data actually correct, complete, reconciled, structurally valid and ready for cutover? Migration assurance can still rely heavily on row counts, spreadsheets and manual reconciliation. When data is incomplete, relationships break or quality issues surface after go-live, the impact can be operational, financial and regulatory. I'm building MAP Nexus to provide a dedicated validation checkpoint — helping teams discover, map and validate migrated data so they can establish correctness before cutover. My background spans data, migration, analytics and data engineering across financial services, pharmaceuticals, housing, government and other enterprise data environments. I'm building and validating MAP Nexus with early teams, while learning where migration assurance is most painful and where organisations need stronger evidence before go-live. The goal: evidence that migrated data is ready before go-live.
