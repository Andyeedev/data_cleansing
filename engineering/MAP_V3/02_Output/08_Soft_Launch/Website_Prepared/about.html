About MAP

Transforming enterprise migration assurance through AI-powered governance and intelligence

Our Mission

To provide enterprise organisations with a trusted, AI-powered platform that ensures migration programmes are delivered with complete governance, assurance, validation, and intelligence — reducing risk, ensuring compliance, and accelerating time to value.

Our Vision

To become the standard platform for enterprise migration assurance — where every migration programme is governed, validated, and delivered with confidence. We envision a world where migration risk is minimised, compliance is automated, and organisations can migrate with certainty.
